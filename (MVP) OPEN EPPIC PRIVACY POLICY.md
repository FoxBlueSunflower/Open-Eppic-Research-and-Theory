# **OPEN EPPIC PRIVACY POLICY (MVP)**

**Effective Date**: \[DATE\]  
 **Version**: 1.0 (MVP \- No Monetization)

---

## **OUR CORE PROMISE**

**We will never sell your personal data.** This is constitutionally protected.

---

## **WHAT WE COLLECT**

**When You Register:**

* Email address  
* Username  
* Password (encrypted)  
* Affirmation of 13+ age  
* Optional: bio, avatar, social links

**When You Use the Platform:**

* Content you create (Works, Elements, comments)  
* Pages you visit, searches, clicks  
* IP address, browser type, device info  
* Cookies for login and basic analytics

**We do NOT collect:**

* Precise GPS location  
* Social media activity  
* Cross-site tracking data

---

## **HOW WE USE YOUR DATA**

**To run the platform:**

* Manage your account  
* Display your public content  
* Enable collaboration  
* Prevent fraud and abuse  
* Send transactional emails (security alerts, policy updates)

**To improve:**

* Analyze usage patterns (anonymized)  
* Fix bugs and optimize performance  
* Test new features

**We will NEVER:**

* Sell your data to third parties  
* Share with advertisers  
* Use your content for AI training without permission  
* Track you across other websites

---

## **WHAT'S PUBLIC**

**Everyone can see:**

* Your username  
* Your bio, avatar, social links  
* Works and Elements you publish  
* Your comments and reviews

**Private:**

* Your email  
* Your real name (unless you choose to display it)  
* Your IP address  
* Your browsing activity

---

## **WHO WE SHARE WITH**

**Service providers only:**

* Cloud hosting (stores encrypted data)  
* Email delivery (for platform emails)  
* Analytics (privacy-focused, anonymized)  
* Content moderation AI (text only, no personal info)

**Legal requirements:**

* Court orders, valid subpoenas  
* Child safety reports (NCMEC)  
* Emergency situations (imminent harm)

All service providers sign confidentiality agreements and can't use your data for their own purposes.

---

## **YOUR RIGHTS**

**You can:**

* Access all your data (email privacy@openeppic.com)  
* Download your data (JSON or CSV)  
* Correct your information (Account Settings)  
* Delete your account (published Works remain per TOS)  
* Opt out of optional emails  
* Disable analytics cookies

**California residents:** Additional CCPA rights apply. Email privacy@openeppic.com with "CCPA Request."

---

## **DATA SECURITY**

We protect your data with:

* HTTPS encryption  
* Encrypted password storage  
* Secure cloud hosting  
* Regular backups  
* Access controls

**If there's a breach:** We'll notify you within 72 hours.

---

## **IMPORTANT STUFF**

* **Age requirement:** 13+ only. Under-13 accounts are terminated immediately.  
* **US-only:** All data stored and processed in United States.  
* **Changes:** We'll notify you 30 days before material changes.  
* **Cookies:** Essential cookies required; analytics cookies optional.  
* **Data retention:** Active accounts kept until deletion; deleted accounts purged in 30 days (published Works remain per TOS).
