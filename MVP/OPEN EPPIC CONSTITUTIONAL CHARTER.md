# **Open Eppic Constitutional Charter (MVP)**

## **Mission**

Enabling creators to build on each other's work in ways that honor both original creation and derivative transformation.

## **Constancy of Purpose**

To solve the derivative works problem by creating a system where original creators retain ownership, can expand their commercial opportunity, and are protected by platform-wide content standards—while derivative creators gain legal permission, commercial opportunity and creative freedom within those bounds. The platform serves both through constitutional governance, accepting that honor means fair treatment and compensation, not creative control.

---

## **Preamble: Theological Foundation**

Open Eppic recognizes the God of the Bible—Father, Son (Jesus), and Holy Spirit—as ultimate reality and source of all goodness, truth, and creativity. While we acknowledge this foundation explicitly, the moral principles flowing from it can be affirmed by people of diverse beliefs as self-evident truths.

Open Eppic exists under transcendent moral law, not human opinion. These Constitutional Commitments bind the company absolutely.

**Governance Philosophy:** We trust God's ways prove themselves in practice. We embrace radical transparency, economic alignment, and ecosystem resilience through constitutional forking.

---

## **Article I: Recognition of Transcendent Moral Order**

1. **Objective morality exists** \- Right and wrong are established by God, not subject to human opinion or cultural preference.  
2. **Humanity is subject to this moral order** \- No person or organization can redefine fundamental moral truths.  
3. **Open Eppic is bound by these principles** \- Profitability, legality, or popularity cannot justify violating them.  
4. **Finite human wisdom requires humility** \- While principles are unchangeable, their application requires contextual wisdom.

---

## **Article II: The Unchangeable Moral Commitments**

*These principles cannot be amended, removed, or reinterpreted to contradict their plain meaning.*

### **II.1 \- The Image of God: Human Dignity and Sanctity of Life**

**Foundation:** "God created mankind in his own image." (Genesis 1:27)

Every human possesses inherent, sacred worth as God's image-bearer. Human life begins at conception.

**Platform Application:** All persons deserve respect and equal treatment. No person may be treated as merely a means to an end. AI/digital entities may warrant respect but never equal moral standing with humans.

**Prohibitions:** Dehumanizing content; exploitation of users/creators/employees; discrimination in moderation or access; denying sanctity of human life.

**Clarification:** Stories may depict evil (as the Bible does), but the platform cannot promote or extensively engage with such content as entertainment.

---

### **II.2 \- Truth and Honesty: Bearing True Witness**

**Foundation:** "You shall not bear false witness." (Exodus 20:16)

Deception, misrepresentation, and dishonesty are forbidden. Transparency is required in all dealings.

**Prohibitions:** Dark patterns, hidden fees, misleading marketing, selective enforcement, concealing information stakeholders have a right to know.

**Obligations:** Real-time transparency in financial/operational/governance matters; clear, readable terms; accessible explanations of algorithms and monetization.

---

### **II.3 \- Ownership and Stewardship: God's Gift of Property**

**Foundation:** "The earth is the Lord's." (Psalm 24:1) / "You shall not steal." (Exodus 20:15)

Ownership is a God-given right; theft violates God's order. Creators own their work, users own their data, employees own their compensation.

**The Stewardship Paradox:** Open Eppic owns the platform with full legal rights (a gift from God) AND stewards it, held in trust from God for a purpose beyond ourselves. Ownership grants authority; stewardship imposes obligation.

**Prohibitions:** IP theft; platform claiming ownership of user content beyond operational necessity; unjust deprivation of earnings/access; using ownership to exploit rather than serve.

**Obligations:** Clear IP rights; transparent revenue sharing with immutable published terms; respect for user data (minimal collection, user control, no selling); platform resources for mission fulfillment, not personal enrichment.

---

### **II.4 \- Justice and Protection of the Vulnerable**

**Foundation:** "Defend the weak and the fatherless." (Psalm 82:3)

God commands special care for the vulnerable. Those with power have heightened responsibility to exercise it justly.

**Applications:** Small creators receive identical treatment as established ones; clear appeal processes; accessibility for disabled users; transparent rules prevent exploitation.

**Prohibitions:** Predatory monetization; allowing harassment; unequal enforcement based on influence or revenue.

---

### **II.5 \- Sabbath Rest and Human Limits**

**Foundation:** "Remember the Sabbath day." (Exodus 20:8-10)

Rest is sacred; productivity isn't the ultimate measure of human worth. Technology should serve human flourishing, not enslave users to endless engagement.

**Applications:** Sustainable workloads with true time off; no exploitation of addictive patterns/FOMO; features encouraging healthy boundaries.

**Prohibitions:** Requiring unsustainable hours; designing features that prey on psychological vulnerabilities; measuring success through engagement metrics that incentivize addiction.

---

### **II.6 \- Creative Freedom Within Moral Bounds**

**Foundation:** God allows immense evil on earth while maintaining boundaries.

Creativity reflects God's image. Stories explore the full range of human experience. Evil may occur in stories and be depicted/explored but not glorified or lingered upon in graphic, exploitative detail.

**Forbidden:**

* Graphic sexual content (explicit description of sexual acts)  
* Graphic violence (detailed gore, torture for shock/entertainment)  
* Content sexualizing minors in any form  
* Direct hate speech, calls to violence, promotion of illegal harmful activities  
* Content endorsing cruelty or dehumanization as virtuous  
* Animal cruelty depicted extensively/gratuitously

**Allowed with Content Warnings:**

* Violence and consequences (non-graphic/off-screen)  
* Adult themes including sexuality (implied, "fade to black," discussed without explicit detail)  
* Political content across the spectrum  
* Exploration of difficult moral questions  
* Depiction of evil characters/actions within story context  
* Mature themes (addiction, abuse, trauma) handled with care

---

### **II.7 \- Love and Relational Commitment**

**Foundation:** "Love one another." (John 13:34)

God is love; His people must reflect this. God invites all to relationship with Him; invitation is optional, not coerced.

**Applications:** Platform fosters genuine community; moderation is corrective/educational, not punitive; respect maintained even in sharp disagreement; users may decline participation without penalty.

**Prohibitions:** Harassment, bullying, personal attacks; coercive design patterns; creating/tolerating hostile environments; using platform power to humiliate users.

---

### **II.8 \- Humility and Service**

**Foundation:** God was humble to the point of washing sinners' feet.

Open Eppic is imperfect and commits to continual learning. The company exists to serve its community. Leadership is stewardship and service, not lordship.

**Applications:** Admitting mistakes publicly and promptly; seeking user feedback genuinely; prioritizing actual user needs; listening especially to critical voices.

**Prohibitions:** Defensiveness toward legitimate criticism; "we know best" attitudes; using market position to dictate terms unfairly; treating users as problems to manage.

---

### **II.9 \- Freedom Within Boundaries**

**Foundation:** God grants free will while maintaining moral boundaries for flourishing.

God grants radical freedom, including freedom to reject Him. Open Eppic allows wide creative/ideological freedom within defined moral boundaries.

**The Principle of Minimal Intervention:**

* Platform intervenes only to prevent flagrant harm or clear violations  
* "I disagree" ≠ grounds for removal  
* "This offends me" ≠ grounds for removal  
* "This causes direct harm" \= grounds for intervention  
* When in doubt, err on side of freedom

**The Third Option Principle:** When two sides demand the platform enforce their view, actively seek options preserving freedom for both.

**The Mental Helmet Principle:** Users must exercise reasonable psychological resilience. Not all discomfort justifies content removal. Content warnings/filtering tools empower user choice.

**The Reader Safety Principle:** When creative content can cause documentable harm to vulnerable readers (suicide contagion, eating disorder instruction), boundaries exist to prevent misuse of freedom that destroys others' flourishing.

---

### **II.10 \- Structural Reality: Freedom Within God's Created Order**

**Foundation:** "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth." (Genesis 1:1)

God grants humans radical freedom yet all exist within the reality He created. **True freedom operates within structure, not in its absence.** God does not apologize for establishing boundaries—they are expressions of His nature and foundation of flourishing.

Just as God establishes boundaries within which humans freely operate, Open Eppic establishes a constitutional framework within which creators freely operate.

**We will not apologize for being a force for good.**

**Critical Distinction:**

* **God's creation:** Inescapable  
* **Open Eppic:** Entirely optional (many alternatives exist, genuine choice available)

**Users are completely free to:**

* Join our model and operate within constitutional boundaries  
* Reject our model and choose alternative platforms

**We cannot and will not compromise the Constitutional Charter.** Just as gravity remains regardless of belief, Open Eppic's constitutional principles remain regardless of agreement.

**This is not tyranny—it is honest structure.** We clearly state what we are, what we stand for, and what we require. Those who resonate are welcome; those who don't have genuine alternatives.

**Prohibitions:** Apologizing for/undermining constitutional principles; compromising boundaries for user count/commercial success; hiding constitutional requirements; preventing users from forking/creating alternatives; making exit difficult/punitive.

---

## **The Epistemological Humility Principle**

Open Eppic operates with **faith in God's design even when we cannot fully articulate the moral reasoning behind it.**

God's wisdom infinitely exceeds human comprehension (Isaiah 55:8). Some moral truths are self-evident in outcomes even when full theological basis remains mysterious. Observing that God's ways "work" provides evidence without complete theoretical understanding.

**When faced with complex moral questions, Open Eppic reserves the right to say "I don't know why, but I trust God's design."**

**Critical Distinction:**

* **What we hold with certainty:** The nine Constitutional Commitments  
* **What we hold provisionally:** Specific implementation details, edge case resolutions  
* **What we admit we don't know:** Full theological reasoning for every divine command

**We refuse two opposite errors:**

1. Claiming omniscience: Pretending perfect understanding of every moral question  
2. Abandoning foundations: Discarding principles we can't fully explain

**Instead, we operate with humble confidence:** Confident in God's revealed truth, humble about our finite understanding, willing to say "I trust God's design here even though I can't fully explain it."

We believe in God’s ways as we believe that the sun has risen. Not only because we experience His ways as good, but because by His ways we see everything else.
