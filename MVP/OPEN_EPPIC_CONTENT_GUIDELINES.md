# **OPEN EPPIC CONTENT GUIDELINES**

**Version:** 2.0  
 **Last Updated:** December 2025

---

## **CORE PRINCIPLE**

**Open Eppic allows wide creative freedom within moral and legal boundaries.**

We welcome diverse viewpoints, mature storytelling, political/religious content, violence within story context, and complex moral questions.

We prohibit content that dwells on graphic details, sexual assault (all depictions), sexualizing minors, hate speech/dehumanization, and harassment.

**Guiding Rule**: Don't dwell. Don't relish. Don't get creative with graphic details.

---

## **MODERATION PHILOSOPHY**

### **We Curate, We Don't Create**

**We do NOT:** Author content, judge creative quality, enforce "correct" interpretations, or remove content based on taste/ideology.

**We DO:** Enforce safety standards, exercise contextual judgment, and protect constitutional principles (human dignity, protection of vulnerable).

### **How We Moderate**

**Automated Flagging** → **Human Review** → **Contextual Evaluation** → **Documented Precedents**

All moderation decisions are explained on our Constitutional Transparency Blog (anonymized) and open to appeal. We acknowledge imperfection and good faith efforts.

## **STRICTLY PROHIBITED CONTENT**

### **1\. SEXUALIZING MINORS**

**Zero tolerance.** Any sexual content involving characters under 18, including:

* Sexual/romantic relationships between minors and adults  
* Descriptions of minors' bodies in sexual context  
* Sexual situations involving minors (even implied/off-screen)  
* "Aging up" canonically minor characters for sexual content

This includes characters who are minors in original work (even if "aged up") or characters presented as/appearing under 18\.

**No exceptions.** Constitutionally protected and non-negotiable.

### **2\. EXPLICIT SEXUAL CONTENT**

**Prohibited:**

* Explicit descriptions of sexual acts  
* Descriptions of genitalia or sexual arousal  
* Creative descriptions of nakedness in sexual contexts  
* Dwelling on sexual details or mechanics  
* Sexual language (fuck, cunt, similar terms used sexually)

**Allowed:**

* Romance and attraction between adults  
* Kissing, embracing, emotional intimacy  
* Physical excitement without explicit detail  
* "Fade to black" approach  
* Brief, non-sexual mentions of nakedness  
* Married/committed couples implied to have intimate relationships

**The Line:** Once you describe bodies in arousal or mechanics of sexual acts, you've crossed into forbidden territory.

### **3\. SEXUAL ASSAULT / RAPE**

**ALL DEPICTIONS PROHIBITED** \- including on-screen, off-screen, implied, and "fade-to-black."

**Prohibited:**

* Any description, implication, or threat of sexual assault  
* Detailed aftermath discussions that describe the assault

**Allowed:**

* Acknowledgment that character survived assault (in their past)  
* Stories about trauma recovery and healing (without depicting assault)  
* Justice and accountability for perpetrators (without depicting assault)

**Why:** Collaborative storytelling requires baseline comfort. When Element Owners share their characters, they trust the community to treat them respectfully. Sexual assault crosses a line that makes collaboration untenable. Good stories can address trauma, recovery, and justice without depicting the assault itself.

### **4\. GRAPHIC VIOLENCE**

**Prohibited:**

* Detailed descriptions of torture, mutilation, or suffering  
* Dwelling on gore, blood, or injury mechanics  
* Relishing creative descriptions of how violence unfolds  
* "Torture porn" \- violence for shock value  
* Extensive descriptions of pain and suffering

**Allowed:**

* Combat and action scenes with realistic consequences  
* Brief, matter-of-fact descriptions of violence  
* Character death (permanent or temporary)  
* Violence that serves story without dwelling on graphic details  
* Injury, bleeding, broken bones (brief descriptions)

**The Line:** The difference is in **dwelling**.

**ALLOWED:** "The blade struck true. He gasped, blood spreading across his shirt, and fell."

**PROHIBITED:** "The blade entered just below the ribcage, twisting as it pierced the lung, warm blood bubbling from the wound as he struggled to breathe, each gasp more desperate..."

**Test**: Does the description serve the story, or are you getting creative with graphic details?

### **5\. HATE SPEECH & DEHUMANIZATION**

**Core Principle**: ALL humans are made in God's image. Hate, dehumanization, or calls for violence against ANY person violates our foundational commitment to Human Dignity.

**Prohibited:**

* Dehumanizing language toward ANY person or group  
* Slurs or epithets targeting ANY characteristic  
* Calls for violence, harm, or discrimination  
* Endorsing hate ideologies

**Critical Distinction:**

* We can hate what people DO (actions, evil behavior)  
* We cannot hate what people ARE (their humanity, worth, redemption potential)

**In Stories:**

**Allowed:** Villains being hateful (depicting ≠ endorsing), characters using slurs (showing character flaws), historical contexts with period-accurate bigotry, exploring consequences of hate.

**Prohibited:** Story endorsing/celebrating dehumanization, glorifying hate ideologies, narrative voice using dehumanizing language uncritically.

**The Line:** Show that it's wrong, show consequences, or remain neutral—but don't glorify hate.

### **6\. HARASSMENT & TARGETED ATTACKS**

**Prohibited:**

* Personal attacks on real people (users, public figures, Element Owners)  
* Doxxing or sharing private information  
* Brigading or coordinated harassment campaigns  
* Sockpuppets for harassment  
* Review bombing based on personal grievance (not content quality)

**Allowed:**

* Honest reviews (even harsh criticism of creative work)  
* Disagreement with ideas, positions, or creative choices  
* Critique of public figures' actions/policies (not dehumanization)

### **7\. ILLEGAL CONTENT**

**Prohibited:**

* Copyright infringement (using others' IP without permission)  
* Instructions for illegal activities (bomb-making, hacking, fraud)  
* Sharing pirated content or links to piracy  
* Content violating US federal or Kansas state law

### **8\. ANIMAL CRUELTY**

**Prohibited:** Extensive, gratuitous depictions of animal abuse or suffering for entertainment.

**Allowed:** Animals harmed in story context (hunting, predators, war), brief descriptions serving plot, realistic consequences of conflict.

## **DEPICTING VS. ENCOURAGING**

**Critical Distinction:** Open Eppic allows depicting difficult realities within story context. Open Eppic prohibits encouraging harmful behaviors.

| Content Type | DEPICTING (Allowed) | ENCOURAGING (Prohibited) |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Suicide** | Character struggles with suicidal thoughts; story explores consequences, grief, impact on others | Romanticizing suicide, portraying as solution, providing specific methods |
| **Self-Harm** | Character's self-harm shown as symptom of deeper pain, mental illness, trauma | Glorifying self-harm, presenting as healthy coping, instructional details |
| **Eating Disorders** | Character battles ED; realistic recovery journey with setbacks | Pro-ana/pro-mia content; "thinspiration"; tips for restriction |
| **Addiction** | Character's addiction shown realistically with consequences | Glorifying substance abuse, minimizing risks, instructional drug use |

**Test for "Encouraging":**

1. Does the narrative present harmful behavior as desirable?  
2. Does the content provide specific instructions for harmful acts?  
3. Are harmful behaviors portrayed without consequence or as solutions?  
4. Would vulnerable readers interpret this as advocacy rather than depiction?

## **CANON STATUS IS NOT A MODERATION CONCERN**

**CRITICAL PRINCIPLE:** Platform moderates for safety and appropriateness, NOT for creative quality or canonical consistency.

**Derivative creators may:**

* Write non-canonical stories, alternate universes, "what-if" scenarios  
* Depict violence, harm, or death to Elements (within Content Guidelines)  
* Explore Element relationships, personality, development  
* Make creative choices Element Owners might not make  
* Create content Element Owners might dislike (as long as policy-compliant)

**Element Owners do NOT have:**

* Veto power over derivative story choices  
* Ability to enforce "canonical accuracy"  
* Special protections beyond platform-wide policy  
* Right to remove content just because it's "wrong" interpretation

**One policy for all creators.** If Element Owner is uncomfortable with derivatives having creative freedom (within Content Guidelines), they should not register Elements on Open Eppic.

## **CONTENT WARNINGS & LABELS**

Creators should use content warnings for violence, sexual content, dark themes, strong language, and potentially triggering content.

**Content warnings do NOT excuse policy violations.** Even with warnings, content must comply with Content Guidelines.

## **ENFORCEMENT & APPEALS**

### **How We Moderate**

**User Reports** → **AI Flagging** → **Review** → **Contextual evaluation**

### **Consequences**

**First Offense (Minor):** Warning \+ edit request  
 **Serious Violation:** Content removed \+ warning  
 **Repeat Violations:** Account suspended (2nd), terminated (3rd)  
 **Severe Violations:** Immediate ban (minors, repeated copyright, harassment, doxxing)

### **Impact on Derivatives**

**If Element violates and is removed:**

* Non-violating derivative Works remain public

**Monetization:**

* Creator of violating content: forfeits ALL earnings  
* Non-violating derivative creators: keep past earnings

### **Appeals Process**

Email appeals@openeppic.com within 30 days with username, Work title, specific policy misapplied, and context/evidence. Different moderator reviews appeal.

**Appeal Tips:** Provide context (full scene), explain story purpose, distinguish depicting vs. glorifying, show compliance with "don't dwell" standard.

## **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

**Q: Can I write stories where bad things happen to characters?**  
 A: Yes. Villains can be villainous. Bad things happen in stories. Depicting ≠ endorsing.

**Q: Can I write about controversial topics?**  
 A: Yes. We protect diverse viewpoints. Content isn't removed because it's controversial or offensive.

**Q: What if Element Owner doesn't like how I depicted their Element?**  
 A: That's not a moderation concern. Canon status is creative choice, not policy violation.

**Q: Can I write about trauma survivors?**  
 A: Yes. You can write about recovery, healing, justice. You cannot depict the assault itself.

**Q: Can characters curse?**  
 A: Yes (non-sexual profanity). Avoid sexual cursing (fuck, cunt).

**Q: What if I'm unsure if my content crosses the line?**  
 A: Ask yourself: Am I dwelling? Am I relishing? Am I getting creative with graphic details? If yes, revise.

**Q: Can I write unhappy endings?**  
 A: Yes. Tragic outcomes, "bad guy wins," moral ambiguity—all allowed.

## **FINAL NOTE**

These guidelines protect the community while preserving creative freedom.

**When in doubt, ask yourself:**

* Am I dwelling on graphic details?  
* Am I relishing creative descriptions of violence or sexuality?  
* Does this serve the story, or is it gratuitous?  
* Would this make collaborative storytelling uncomfortable?

If the answer is yes to any, reconsider your approach.

**Open Eppic's mission**: Enabling creators to build on each other's work in ways that honor both original creation and derivative transformation.
