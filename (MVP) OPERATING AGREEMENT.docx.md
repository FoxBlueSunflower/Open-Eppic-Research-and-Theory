# **ARTICLE II: CONSTITUTIONAL FOUNDATION**

## **2.1 Governing Authority**

This Company operates under the authority of the God of the Bible. Objective morality exists—established by God, not human opinion. The Constitutional Commitments outlined in Section 2.2 are absolute and unchangeable.

## **2.2 The Nine Constitutional Commitments**

The Company is bound by the following immutable principles:

1. **Human Dignity** – Every person bears God's image with sacred worth from conception onward. No dehumanization, exploitation, or discrimination.  
2. **Truth & Honesty** – Radical transparency required. No deception tolerated. Open operations, clear terms, kept promises. No dark patterns or hidden fees.  
3. **Ownership & Stewardship** – Property rights (including intellectual property) must be respected. No IP theft, unfair deprivation, or exploitation.  
4. **Justice for the Vulnerable** – Special protection for those who cannot defend themselves. Equal treatment for all creators, clear appeals processes.  
5. **Sabbath Rest** – Technology serves human flourishing, not addiction. Sustainable work, healthy boundaries. No exploitation of compulsive behaviors.  
6. **Creative Freedom Within Bounds** – Wide artistic freedom within moral and legal boundaries. Content policy enforces reasonable standards comparable to published fiction.  
7. **Love & Community** – Everything done with love, not cruelty. Corrective moderation, genuine community. No harassment, coercion, or hostile environments.  
8. **Humility & Service** – The Company exists to serve its community. Admit mistakes publicly, incorporate feedback, prioritize user needs.  
9. **Freedom Within Boundaries** – Minimal intervention; wide ideological freedom within moral guardrails. Protect diverse viewpoints, consistent rules, user agency.

## **2.3 Immutability**

These Constitutional Commitments cannot be amended, modified, or removed. Any attempt to do so triggers the stewardship protection mechanisms in Article IV.

## **2.4 Constitutional Charter**

The detailed applications, prohibitions, and theological foundations for these Constitutional Commitments are set forth in the "Open Eppic Constitutional Charter," which is incorporated into this Operating Agreement by reference. Any conflict between this Operating Agreement and the Charter shall be resolved in favor of protecting the Constitutional Commitments.

# **ARTICLE III: MEMBERSHIP & GOVERNANCE**

## **3.2 Golden Share Structure**

The Company maintains a dual-class structure:

**Class A Units:** Voting and economic rights for all Company matters.

**Class B Golden Share:** Single unit held by Open Eppic Purpose Trust with veto power over constitutional violations. Zero economic value, cannot be transferred or diluted.

### **3.2.1 Constitutional Veto Authority**

The Golden Share holder has veto authority over:

(a) Amendments to Constitutional Commitments

(b) Sale of user data to third parties

(c) Actions systematically violating Constitutional Commitments

(d) Dissolution, sale, or merger of the Company

(e) Any other matter deemed to threaten the constitutional mission

# **ARTICLE IV: STEWARDSHIP ENFORCEMENT**

## **4.1 Call Option**

All Class A Members voluntarily grant the Open Eppic Purpose Trust a Call Option to purchase their membership units at $0.01 per unit if constitutional violations occur. This option may be exercised within 90 days of violation notice.

## **4.2 The Open Eppic Purpose Trust**

The Purpose Trust is a Kansas Charitable Trust established to:

(a) Hold the Golden Share permanently

(b) Monitor constitutional compliance

(c) Exercise veto authority when violations occur

(d) Exercise the Call Option if necessary to preserve the mission

### **4.2.1 Trustee Structure**

The Trust is governed by \[specify number\] Trustees representing theological expertise and community interests. Trustees serve staggered terms and cannot be removed by Class A Members.

## **4.3 Fork Provision**

If the Company fundamentally changes its mission, any member may fork the codebase and Constitutional Charter to create an alternative platform. The Company commits to maintaining open-source transparency to enable this provision.

# **ARTICLE V: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & LICENSING**

## **5.1 Creator Ownership**

Creators retain full ownership of all content they create on the platform. The Company claims no ownership rights to user-generated content beyond the operational license granted in Section 5.2.

## **5.2 Operational License**

By publishing content on Open Eppic, creators grant the Company a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to host, display, distribute, and technically reproduce their content solely for platform operations. This license terminates upon content deletion, except for archived copies necessary for platform integrity.

## **5.3 Element Registration System**

Creators may register original story components ("Elements") including:

(a) Characters

(b) Settings and locations

(c) Magic systems and worldbuilding rules

(d) Plot structures and story frameworks

(e) Items, artifacts, and story-specific concepts

### **5.3.1 Element Requirements**

To be eligible for registration, Elements must:

(a) Be original creations or properly licensed from external sources

(b) Be sufficiently specific (not generic archetypes)

(c) Include clear usage guidelines

(d) Comply with Content Guidelines (Article VI)

## **5.4 Element Licensing**

When a creator uses another creator's registered Element in their Work, they accept the Element owner's licensing terms. Element owners may specify creative guidelines but cannot prohibit derivative works entirely once an Element is published on the platform.

## **5.5 Perpetual License**

Once a Work is published using licensed Elements, the license becomes perpetual and irrevocable for that specific Work. Element owners cannot retroactively revoke licenses for published Works.

## **5.6 Public Domain and External IP**

Creators may freely use public domain works and external intellectual property where legally permitted. The Company takes no responsibility for ensuring creators have proper rights to use external IP—that responsibility rests solely with the creator.

# **ARTICLE VI: CONTENT POLICY**

## **6.1 Content Standards**

Open Eppic maintains content standards comparable to published fiction (PG-13 to R-rated). The platform balances creative freedom with safety, recognizing that stories must explore difficult themes while avoiding gratuitous harm.

## **6.2 Forbidden Content**

The following content is prohibited in all circumstances:

* Graphic sexual content (explicit description of sexual acts)  
* Graphic violence (detailed gore, torture for shock value)  
* Content sexualizing minors in any form  
* Direct hate speech or calls to violence  
* Promotion of illegal harmful activities  
* Animal cruelty depicted extensively or gratuitously

## **6.3 Allowed Content (with appropriate warnings)**

The following content is permitted when handled appropriately:

* Violence within story context (non-graphic, off-screen)  
* Adult themes including sexuality (implied, "fade to black")  
* Political content across the ideological spectrum  
* Exploration of difficult moral questions  
* Depiction of evil characters and actions within story context  
* Mature themes (addiction, abuse, trauma) handled with care

## **6.4 Moderation Philosophy**

Content moderation operates on the following principles:

(a) "I disagree" or "This offends me" does not constitute grounds for removal

(b) Canon status is not a moderation concern—quality does not determine safety

(c) Moderation is corrective and educational, not punitive

(d) Clear appeal processes exist for all moderation decisions

(e) When in doubt, err on the side of freedom
