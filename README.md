# Open Eppic

**The mission and governance home for a constitutional framework for collaborative storytelling.**

Open Eppic exists to answer one question: how can creators legally build on each other's work — without one party exploiting the other, and without a platform quietly rewriting the deal later?

This repository is where that mission lives: the principles it's built on, the governance that protects it, and the record of decisions about where it goes next.

---

## The Mission

Fanfiction culture proves collaborative storytelling works. It also proves the legal and economic model underneath it is broken — derivative creators risk takedowns, original creators lose control and compensation, and platforms have historically claimed ownership of everyone's work (see: Kindle Worlds).

Open Eppic's goal is a framework where:

- **Original creators** register story components ("Elements") and keep ownership of them.
- **Derivative creators** get real, perpetual licenses to build on those Elements — not a legal gray area.
- **Governance is constitutional**, not a terms-of-service page that can change on a whim.
- **The whole thing is forkable**, on purpose, so the mission survives even if any one steward doesn't.

## Governance

The project is steered by a small set of commitments meant to outlast any single implementation:

- **Ownership stays with creators.** The platform stewards the mission; it does not own the work.
- **Transparency by default.** Decisions, tradeoffs, and reasoning are kept in version control, not behind closed doors.
- **Constitutional, not contractual.** Core principles are meant to bind the platform itself, enforceable by structure, not just by good intentions.
- **Forkable infrastructure.** If Open Eppic ever betrays its own principles, the community can take the framework and rebuild elsewhere.

These commitments are being actively refined. Treat anything not marked final as a working draft, not a settled spec.

## Where things stand

Open Eppic is in an early, active-rethink phase. The detailed legal and product work in the archive (below) was produced before the project adopted W. Edwards Deming's systems-thinking approach to its own process, and much of it is being revisited in that light. Going forward, this repository is meant to hold:

- The evolving constitutional and governance principles for the platform.
- Decisions and their rationale, kept in the open.
- Pointers to the archive when historical context is useful.

**Status: seeking collaborators.** If this mission resonates — developers, IP-minded legal reviewers, community builders, or people who just want to think hard about governance — open an issue or reach out.

## The Archive

Earlier drafts of the legal framework, product spec, and strategic planning are preserved for history in [`archive/world-community-era/`](archive/world-community-era/). That material — constitutional charter drafts, terms of service, operating agreement, creator guides, and the original "why this matters" writeup — represents real work and real thinking, but it predates the project's current direction and should not be treated as current policy.

It's kept, not deleted, because governance history matters: future decisions should be traceable to what came before and why it changed.
