# Open Eppic Monetization Guide

**Version 2.0 | Updated: December 2025**

---

## The Core Concept

Open Eppic is a collaborative storytelling platform where creators earn from their work and readers pay for what they value. Creators make **Works** (stories, artwork) using **Elements** (characters, settings, systems, items, plots) created by other creators. Revenue is split fairly, transparently, and automatically.

**Key Principle**: Platform takes 20%. Creators and Element owners collectively keep 80%. All splits are locked at time of publication and fully transparent.

---

## What Can Be Monetized

### Monetizable Content

**Works** – Stories or artwork that combine Elements into a single creation

- Full novels, short stories, serialized fiction  
- Artwork, illustrations, visual narratives  
- Price: $0.99-$9.99 per Work (creator sets)

**Chapters** – Sequential parts of serialized Works

- Individual chapters can be sold separately or bundled  
- Price: $0.49-$2.99 per chapter

**Original Elements** – Unique, detailed story components that can be reused:

- **Characters**: Entities that can modify or create physical/informational aspects  
- **Settings**: Locations or environments (any scale from cosmic to microscopic)  
- **Items**: Physical or informational objects that move but cannot independently modify/create  
- **Systems**: Collections of rules defining how something works (magic systems, governance systems)  
- **Plots**: Highly specific, detailed story structures (not generic archetypes)

**Requirements**: Elements must be original, unique, detailed (200+ words), and clearly defined.

**Element Deep Lore** – Extended backstories and usage notes

- Optional detailed content for your Elements  
- Price: $0.99-$4.99 per Element

### Non-Monetizable Content

**Derivative Elements** – Elements based on others' original Elements

- Do NOT receive revenue splits directly  
- Revenue flows to the original Element(s) they're based on  
- Created for community formation and discovery, not direct monetization  
- You CAN earn creator revenue (40-80%) by writing Works using your derivative

**Generic Archetypes** – Universal story patterns

- "The Hero's Journey" is not monetizable  
- "The Reluctant Hero" is not monetizable  
- Original, detailed characters BASED on archetypes ARE monetizable

**Brands** – Collections of principles, ideas, Works, and Elements

- Not directly monetizable on the platform  
- Built through successful Works and Elements

## How Creators Earn Money

### 1\. Work Sales (Direct Revenue)

When you sell a Work or Chapter:

**If using NO Elements owned by others (100% original):**

- **You receive**: 80% of sale price  
- **Platform receives**: 20%

**If using Elements owned by other creators:**

- **You receive**: 40% of sale price  
- **Element owners collectively receive**: 40% (distributed via share system)  
- **Platform receives**: 20%

**You control pricing**: Set your Work as free or paid ($0.99-$9.99)

### 2\. Element Usage Revenue (Passive Income)

When other creators use YOUR Elements in their Works:

- You earn a share of their Work sales  
- This is passive income – earn while others create  
- The more your Elements are used, the more you earn  
- Revenue share depends on Element classification (Small IP or Big IP)

### 3\. Optional: Element Licensing Fees

You can charge creators a one-time fee to unlock your Element for use:

- Fee range: $2.99-$19.99 (one-time payment)  
- Once purchased, they can use it in unlimited Works  
- **You receive**: 80% of licensing fee  
- **Platform receives**: 20%  
- You STILL earn Element usage revenue when your Element is used in monetized Works

**Note**: Free licensing often generates more total revenue through widespread usage than upfront fees.

### 4\. Optional: Deep Lore Sales

Sell extended backstories and detailed notes for your Elements:

- **You receive**: 80% of Deep Lore sales  
- **Platform receives**: 20%  
- Deep Lore is optional – buyers can use your Element without it

## Understanding the Revenue Split System

### The 40/40/20 Structure

When a Work uses Elements owned by other creators, revenue is split:

- **40%** to the Work creator  
- **40%** to Element owners (distributed via share system)  
- **20%** to Platform

**Constitutional Cap**: Platform fee may not exceed 30% of transaction value under any circumstances. Current 20% is operational decision; any increase requires Purpose Trust approval if exceeding 30%.

### How the 40% Element Pool Is Divided

The Element Pool is split into two buckets based on Element classification:

**Small IP Bucket**: 25% of Element Pool (= 10% of total sale)

- For Elements created by independent creators  
- Each Small IP Element \= **1 share**  
- Split equally among all Small IP Elements used in the Work

**Big IP Bucket**: 75% of Element Pool (= 30% of total sale)

- For Elements from professionally-published intellectual properties  
- Each Big IP Element \= **1-30 shares** (set by Platform based on franchise size)  
- Split proportionally based on share values

**Constitutional Protection**: Small IP always receives minimum 10% of total sale when Big IP is present. This protects independent creators from being overwhelmed by major franchises.

### Distribution Rules

**If Work uses ONLY Small IP Elements:**

- Small IP Elements receive full 40% Element Pool  
- Split equally (1 share each)

**If Work uses ONLY Big IP Elements:**

- Big IP Elements receive full 40% Element Pool  
- Split proportionally by share weights

**If Work uses BOTH Small IP and Big IP Elements:**

- Small IP bucket: 10% of sale (25% of Element Pool)  
- Big IP bucket: 30% of sale (75% of Element Pool)  
- Each bucket splits separately

## Derivative Elements: Community Without Direct Revenue

### What Are Derivative Elements?

Derivative Elements build upon existing original Elements. They exist for community formation and discovery, NOT direct monetization.

**Key Distinction:**

- **Original Elements**: Receive revenue splits when used in Works  
- **Derivative Elements**: Do NOT receive revenue splits

**Examples:**

- "Vampire Harry Potter" (based on Harry Potter)  
- "Detective in Middle-earth" (based on LOTR setting)  
- "Cyberpunk Magic System" (based on existing magic system)

### Why Create Derivative Elements?

**Community Benefits:**

- Works using your derivative cluster together in discovery  
- Creators find your unique interpretation  
- You build reputation as creative transformer  
- Enable fanfic-style communities around popular IP

**Monetization Path:**

- Create Works using your derivative → earn 40-80% creator revenue  
- Build community around your interpretation  
- Establish your creative voice

**NOT a Revenue Path:**

- Derivative Elements receive $0 in element splits  
- Revenue flows to original sources you declared  
- This is contractual (you agree when creating derivative)

### How to Create Derivative Elements

**Step 1: Declare Original Sources**

You must declare which original Elements your derivative is based on:

- Can declare multiple originals  
- Originals can be mix of Small IP and Big IP  
- **Cannot declare other derivatives as sources** (no nesting)

**Step 2: Write Unique Description (200+ words)**

Describe what makes YOUR derivative unique:

- How does it transform the original?  
- What new qualities does it have?  
- How should creators use it?

**Step 3: Acknowledge Revenue Flow**

When registering, you acknowledge:

- "Revenue flows to original sources, not to me"  
- Platform shows original sources and revenue implications  
- You understand this is for community, not direct monetization

## Revenue Thresholds

### Two-Tier Threshold System

To prevent administrative overhead from tiny payments:

**Tier 1: Work-Level Threshold ($10/month)**

- Work must earn **$10 per month** before Element splits are calculated  
- Below $10/month → Creator keeps full 80%, no Element splits  
- Above $10/month → Splits calculated and allocated

**Why**: Not worth calculating splits for low-earning Works. Keeps accounting simple.

**Tier 2: Account Payout Threshold ($25 total)**

- Your account balance must reach **$25** for payout  
- Below $25 → Balance accumulates month-to-month  
- At $25+ → Payment processed via Stripe within 30 days

**Annual Sweep**: Once per year (December), balances **≥$5** are paid out even if below $25

**Why**: Reduces payment processing fees by \~87% (1 payment of $25 instead of 25 payments of $1)

### Example Timeline

- **January**: Your Work earns $8 → You receive $6.40 (80%), Elements get $0 (below threshold)  
- **February**: Your Work earns $50 → Splits calculated:  
  - You allocated $20 (40%)  
  - 3 Element owners allocated $6.67 each (added to their balances)  
  - Platform gets $10  
- **March**: Element owner's balance reaches $27 → Payment processed within 30 days  
- **December**: Your balance is $7 (never hit $25) → Swept and paid

## Payment Terms and Process

### Payment Processing

**All payments processed through Stripe Connect**

Before receiving payments, you must complete Stripe Connect onboarding:

- **Identity verification**: Stripe verifies you're 18+ and confirms identity  
- **Tax information**: Stripe collects W-9 (SSN or EIN) for IRS reporting  
- **Bank account/PayPal**: Set up where you want to receive money

**Open Eppic never sees your SSN, Tax ID, or government-issued ID.** All sensitive information handled securely by Stripe.

### Limited Agent Payment Structure

**How It Works:**

When a reader purchases your Work:

1. You are **deemed paid immediately** upon Platform receiving payment  
2. Platform holds funds **in trust** for you during settlement period (NET-30)  
3. Platform acts as your **limited payment collection agent**, not as principal  
4. Platform authorized to deduct Platform Fee (20%, max 30%) before distributing

**Why This Structure:**

- Complies with state Money Transmitter laws  
- Avoids expensive licensing in 40+ states  
- Follows precedent from ride-sharing and delivery platforms  
- Platform is agent (holding your money) not principal (owning your money)

**Merchant of Record:**

- Platform's name appears on buyer's credit card statement  
- Platform handles refunds, chargebacks, disputes  
- Platform issues IRS Form 1099-NEC to you annually  
- You remain responsible for your own tax obligations

### Payout Schedule

**Assessment**: Monthly (earnings calculated at end of each month) **Payout timing**: NET-30 (payment within 30 days of month-end) **Minimum threshold**: $25 (or $5+ in December annual sweep) **Payment method**: Bank transfer or PayPal via Stripe **Currency**: USD only

### Tax Reporting

**IRS Form 1099-NEC:**

- Issued to creators earning **$600+ per calendar year**  
- Delivered electronically by **January 31** via Stripe  
- Stripe handles all W-9 collection and 1099 generation automatically

**Your Tax Responsibilities:**

- Provide accurate tax information during Stripe onboarding  
- Report all earnings on your tax return (use 1099-NEC form)  
- Pay applicable income and self-employment taxes  
- Consult tax professional with questions

**Platform's Role:**

- Platform is legally responsible for issuing 1099-NEC forms  
- Uses Stripe's automated tax service  
- Does NOT receive or store your SSN/Tax ID  
- Tracks earnings totals only (not personal tax information)

### Example: Full Payment Cycle

1. **January 1-31**: You earn $15 from Work sales, account balance reaches $28  
2. **January 31**: Platform assesses, balance exceeds $25 threshold  
3. **February 28**: Transfer of $28 processed to your Stripe account (within 30 days)  
4. **March 1**: You withdraw $28 from Stripe to your bank  
5. **March 1**: New account balance starts at $0

**Tax Season (Annual):**

- **November**: Stripe emails you to confirm tax information for 1099  
- **January 31**: 1099-NEC form delivered electronically via Stripe  
- **April 15**: File your tax return including 1099-NEC income

## Anti-Gaming Protection

### The 10+ Element Review

To prevent coordinated gaming (friends artificially inflating each other's revenue):

**Automatic Review Trigger:**

- Work uses **10+ Elements from a single creator** (excluding your own Elements)  
- Constitutional Guardian reviews before monetization enabled

**Review Process:**

- Guardian verifies Elements are actually used in the Work content  
- Review typically completed within 2-3 business days  
- Most legitimate Works approved immediately

**Purpose:**

- Prevents "spam element" attacks (attaching unused Elements to dilute splits)  
- Allows legitimate collaboration (shared universes, anthologies, worldbuilding partnerships)

**User Experience:**

1. You submit Work using 12 Elements from Alice  
2. Email: "Your Work is under brief review (anti-gaming check)"  
3. Guardian reviews within 2-3 days  
4. **If legitimate**: "Your Work is approved for monetization"  
5. **If gaming detected**: "Elements listed are not actually used in content" with details

**Consequences of Gaming:**

- **First offense**: Work rejected, warning issued  
- **Second offense**: Account suspension (30 days)  
- **Third offense**: Permanent ban  
- **Coordinated gaming**: Both parties may be permanently banned

**Why 10-Element Threshold:**

This catches reciprocal arrangements (main gaming vector) while minimizing false positives for legitimate collaboration.

Prevents creators from attaching spam Elements to their own Works to dilute other Element creators' shares.

## Grandfathering of Revenue Splits

### Revenue Stability Guarantee

**CRITICAL PRINCIPLE:**

Once a Work is published, its revenue split is **locked indefinitely**. There is no expiration date.

Future changes to revenue distribution model do NOT apply to already-published Works.

**Example:**

- Work published in 2025 under 40/40/20 split  
- Platform changes to 50/30/20 split in 2027  
- 2025 Work continues using 40/40/20 **forever**

**Rationale**: Protects creator investment, prevents retroactive harm (Constitutional Commitment \#2: Truth & Honesty)

### Platform Revenue Split Changes

**Minor Changes (Platform share ±5%):**

- 90 days advance notice  
- Detailed explanation of business necessity  
- Community feedback period

**Major Changes (Platform share \>5%):**

- 90 days advance notice  
- Constitutional Guardian Board approval required  
- Community feedback period  
- User referendum if Platform share increases \>10% (60% approval)

**Hard Cap (Constitutional):**

- Platform share cannot exceed **30%** (absolute maximum)  
- Platform share can be lowered anytime without approval  
- Creator \+ Element share must always be **≥70%**

**Small IP/Big IP Bucket Ratio Changes:**

- Require Constitutional Guardian approval  
- Small IP bucket cannot drop below 20% of Element Pool without user referendum  
- Protects independent creators from overwhelming by major franchises

### Your Options if You Disagree

If Platform changes splits for new Works:

- **Your existing Works unaffected** (grandfathered indefinitely)  
- Continue publishing new Works under new split (if acceptable)  
- Cease publishing new Works (existing Works keep earning)  
- Remove your Elements per commitment process (if outside commitment periods)

**You cannot demand Platform revert to old splits**, but your existing Works remain protected forever.

## External Licensing: Using Community Elements Outside Open Eppic

### Taking Platform Elements to Traditional Publishing

If you want to publish work externally (traditional book deal, self-publishing, film adaptation) that incorporates **community-created Elements**, you must obtain external licenses separate from Platform licensing.

**Platform license is non-exclusive and limited to Platform use only.**

### Current Process (Manual Negotiation)

**Step 1: Identify What You Need**

- Which community Elements do you want to use?  
- How significant is each to your external work?  
- Are you using ideas only, or specific expression?

**Step 2: Contact Creators**

- Reach out via Platform or email  
- Explain your project and how you'll use their Elements  
- Propose terms: one-time fee, revenue share, or both

**Step 3: Negotiate Fairly**

- Offer fair compensation (Constitutional Commitment: Justice for Vulnerable)  
- Suggested range: $100-$1,000 per Element \+ 2-5% revenue share  
- Always include attribution credit  
- Be reasonable—don't exploit power imbalance

**Step 4: Document Agreement**

- Written contract strongly recommended (even simple email acceptable)  
- Specify: what you're licensing, how you'll use it, payment terms, credit  
- Both parties sign and retain copies

**Step 5: Execute**

- Pay agreed compensation  
- Provide attribution in external work  
- Honor terms throughout publication

### Ideas vs. Expression Distinction

**If you're only using general ideas** (themes, concepts, tropes) from derivatives:

- No license required (ideas are not copyrightable)  
- Optional: Provide "Inspired By" attribution (recognition without payment)

**If you're using specific Elements or expression**:

- License required  
- Fair compensation required  
- Legal protection for both parties

**When in doubt, license.** Protects everyone and maintains community trust.

### Future: External Licensing Tool

Open Eppic will implement a tool to streamline external licensing:

- Standardized terms and pricing  
- Built-in negotiation platform  
- Automated contracts and payments  
- Legal protection for both parties

**Estimated implementation**: 1,000+ active creators or first licensing need arises.

**Why This Matters**: External licensing is **strategically critical** as Open Eppic scales. Indie creators will engage fluidly with each other's Elements. Making external licensing simple and fair is key to platform success.

Until then, negotiate directly—but always fairly and transparently.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: Do I have to charge for my Work?** A: No. You can make everything free. Free Works still generate Element usage data for discovery.

**Q: Can I change pricing later?** A: Yes. You control pricing for all your Works and Elements at any time.

**Q: What if I use someone's free Element?** A: They still earn their element split when your Work is monetized (even if Element was free to use). This encourages generous sharing.

**Q: Does the Platform own my Work?** A: No. You retain full ownership. Platform only has license to display and distribute. This is constitutionally protected.

---

### Revenue and Payments

**Q: How do I get paid?** A: Via Stripe Connect to bank account or PayPal. NET-30 (within 30 days of earnings). Minimum payout: $25 (or $5+ in December).

**Q: What happens if I'm unreachable for payment?** A: Revenue held for 1 year with email notifications. After 1 year of no contact, funds donated to Project Gutenberg.

**Q: Can I monetize a generic archetype like "The Reluctant Hero"?** A: No. Generic archetypes are not monetizable. But you CAN create an original, detailed character BASED on that archetype (with unique backstory, abilities, personality) and monetize that.

**Q: What if someone uses my Element without licensing it?** A: Platform tracks Element usage automatically. Report unlicensed use to [support@openeppic.com](mailto:support@openeppic.com). Element splits are calculated automatically once usage is detected.

---

### Elements and Derivatives

**Q: Should I create a Derivative Element or Original Element?** A: **Derivative** if you want to build on popular IP for community (forgo direct Element revenue). **Original** if your Element is sufficiently unique (receive Element revenue splits).

**Q: Can I convert my Derivative to Original later?** A: Yes, if it becomes sufficiently distinct. Requirements: 500+ words unique description, demonstrated uniqueness, Constitutional Guardian approval.

**Q: What happens if an Original Element I based my Derivative on gets removed?** A: Your derivative becomes "orphaned" – cannot be used in NEW Works, but existing Works remain published. You may petition to convert to Original status.

**Q: How do I apply for Big IP status?** A: Email [support@openeppic.com](mailto:support@openeppic.com) with evidence of professional publication, commercial success, media coverage, and requested share value (1-30). Review within 14 business days.

**Q: What counts as "gaming the system"?** A: Listing Elements without actually using them in your Work content, or coordinating with friends to artificially inflate each other's revenue through fake Elements.

**Q: What if there's an error in my revenue calculation?** A: Contact [support@openeppic.com](mailto:support@openeppic.com) within 90 days with Work ID and details. Investigation within 14 business days. Errors corrected with supplemental payment.

**Q: Can Platform change the revenue split on my existing Work?** A: No. Your Work's split is locked indefinitely from publication. Changes only affect new Works created after the change.
