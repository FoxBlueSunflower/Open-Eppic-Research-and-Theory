## INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Open Eppic. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

**Constitutional Commitment**: Open Eppic operates under immutable Constitutional Commitments protected by stewardship ownership. **We will never sell your personal data to third parties.** This is not just policy—it's a constitutional principle enforced by our governance structure. Violating this would trigger dissolution.

**Our Promise**: We believe in radical transparency. This Privacy Policy is written in plain language so you understand exactly what happens to your information.

## 1\. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 1.1 Information You Provide

**Account Registration:**

- Email address (required)  
- Username/display name (required)  
- Password (encrypted—we never see your actual password)  
- Date of birth (required for age verification, 18+ only)  
- Optional profile information (bio, avatar, social links)

**Content You Create:**

- Works (stories, novels, series)  
- Elements (characters, settings, items, systems, plots)  
- Comments, reviews, ratings  
- Support communications

**Payment Information (Monetizing Creators Only):**

Before receiving payments, you complete identity verification through Stripe Identity:

- Name, address, date of birth  
- Tax identification (SSN or EIN)  
- Bank account or PayPal  
- Payment method details

**CRITICAL**: Open Eppic never receives or stores your Social Security Number, Tax ID, or government-issued ID documents. All sensitive identity information is handled securely by Stripe. We receive only verification status (verified/not verified).

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Usage Data:**

- Pages visited, Works viewed or purchased  
- Search queries, time on pages, links clicked  
- Features used, interactions with platform

**Device Information:**

- IP address (general location, not precise GPS)  
- Browser type and version  
- Operating system, device type  
- Screen resolution, language preferences

**Cookies:**

- Session cookies (keep you logged in)  
- Preference cookies (remember settings)  
- Analytics cookies (understand platform usage)  
- We do NOT use advertising or tracking cookies

**Location:**

- General location (country/state) from IP address  
- We do NOT collect precise GPS coordinates

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties

**Payment Processor (Stripe):**

**What Stripe Collects from You:**

- Name, email, address, date of birth  
- Tax ID (SSN or EIN via W-9)  
- Bank account or PayPal details  
- Government-issued ID for verification

**What Open Eppic Receives from Stripe:**

- Verification status (verified/not verified)  
- Connected account ID (for transfers)  
- Payout status (completed/pending/failed)  
- Payment method type and last 4 digits  
- Tax form filing status

**What Open Eppic Does NOT Receive:**

- Your Social Security Number or Tax ID  
- Your government ID images or documents  
- Your full bank account number  
- Your W-9 form contents

**Tax Reporting:**

- Stripe collects W-9 during onboarding  
- Stripe securely stores tax IDs  
- Platform uses Stripe's automated 1099-NEC service  
- Platform tracks earnings totals only (not tax IDs)  
- Stripe delivers 1099-NEC electronically by January 31 if you earn $600+ per year

**Stripe Privacy Policy**: stripe.com/privacy

This is constitutionally protected—violating this would trigger stewardship intervention and potential dissolution.

## 3\. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

### 3.1 Public Information

**Publicly Visible to All Users:**

- Username/display name  
- Bio and avatar (if provided)  
- Social links (if provided)  
- Content you've posted (Works, Elements)  
- Comments and reviews  
- Public follower/following counts

**NOT Public:**

- Your email address  
- Your real name (unless you display it)  
- Payment information  
- Tax information  
- Purchase history  
- IP address or precise location  
- Private messages (if feature enabled)

### 3.2 Service Providers

We share limited information with trusted service providers:

**Payment Processing:**

- **Stripe**: Processes payments, verifies identity, handles payouts, issues 1099s  
- Receives: Name, email, payment method, tax ID, payout preferences  
- Why: Required for payment processing, tax compliance, identity verification  
- Privacy Policy: stripe.com/privacy

**Hosting and Infrastructure:**

- **Cloud hosting** (e.g., AWS, DigitalOcean): Stores platform databases and files (encrypted at rest)  
- Subject to strict confidentiality agreements

**Analytics:**

- **Privacy-focused analytics** (e.g., Plausible, Simple Analytics—NOT Google Analytics)  
- Receives: Anonymized usage data only (no personal identifiers)  
- Does NOT track across sites

**Email Delivery:**

- **Transactional email service** (e.g., SendGrid, Mailgun)  
- Receives: Email address, message content for delivery only  
- Used only for emails we send (not marketing lists)

**Content Moderation:**

- **Hive.ai** (or equivalent): AI-powered content flagging for policy violations  
- Receives: Text content for analysis only  
- No personal identifiers shared

**All service providers:**

- Sign confidentiality agreements  
- Limited to necessary data only  
- Prohibited from using data for their own purposes  
- Must maintain security standards equivalent to ours

### 3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law:

**Legal Obligations:**

- Comply with court orders, subpoenas, or legal process  
- Respond to law enforcement requests (after verifying legitimacy)  
- Report suspected child exploitation (NCMEC)  
- Defend our legal rights in litigation

**IRS Tax Reporting:**

- Earnings totals reported to IRS for creators earning $600+ per year  
- Stripe issues 1099-NEC (we don't share tax IDs)

**Emergency Situations:**

- Prevent imminent harm to person or property  
- Protect safety of users or public

We will notify you of legal requests unless prohibited by law or court order.

### 3.4 Business Transfers

If Open Eppic is acquired, merged, or sells assets:

**Constitutional Protection:**

- Operating Agreement requires acquirer to adopt Constitutional Commitments  
- If acquirer refuses, Purpose Trust may trigger dissolution  
- Your data protected by same constitutional principles

**Your Options:**

- You'll be notified 30 days in advance  
- You may delete your account before transfer  
- Published Works remain (perpetual license granted)

### 3.5 Payment Processor Flexibility

**We reserve right to change payment processors** (Stripe, PayPal, direct ACH, cryptocurrency, or other methods) to:

(a) Reduce transaction costs  
(b) Comply with processor requirements  
(c) Ensure operational sustainability  
(d) Maintain constitutional alignment

**Changes announced 30 days in advance** with explanation on Constitutional Transparency Blog.

**Data Implications:**

- New processor will receive necessary payment/identity data  
- You'll be notified of new processor's privacy policy  
- No change to our commitments (we still never sell your data)

**Constitutional Commitment:** If payment processors terminate service due to our constitutional principles (not content violations), we will migrate to alternative infrastructure rather than compromise our values.

### 4.2 Correction and Deletion

**You have the right to:**

**Correct Inaccurate Data:**

- Update profile information in Account Settings  
- Contact [support@openeppic.com](mailto:support@openeppic.com) for data we cannot edit directly

**Delete Your Account:**

**What Cannot Be Deleted:**

### 4.3 Opt-Out Rights

**Marketing Communications:**

- Unsubscribe link in every optional email  
- Account Settings → Email Preferences

**Analytics Cookies:**

- Account Settings → Privacy → Cookie Preferences  
- Browser settings (may affect platform functionality)

**Data Sale (Already Prohibited):**

- We never sell your data, so no opt-out needed  
- Constitutionally protected prohibition

### 4.4 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights:

**Right to Know:**

- Categories of personal information collected  
- Sources of information  
- Business purpose for collection  
- Categories of third parties we share with

**Right to Delete:**

- Request deletion of personal information (with exceptions for legal requirements)

**Right to Opt-Out:**

- We don't sell personal information, so opt-out not applicable

**Right to Non-Discrimination:**

- We won't discriminate for exercising CCPA rights

**How to Exercise:**

- Email [privacy@openeppic.com](mailto:privacy@openeppic.com) with subject "CCPA Request \- \[Specific Right\]"  
- We'll respond within 45 days  
- No charge for first two requests per year

### 4.5 Do Not Track

We honor Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals:

- When DNT enabled, we disable analytics cookies  
- Essential cookies remain (required for login/security)

## 5\. DATA SECURITY

### 5.1 Security Measures

**We protect your data with:**

**Encryption:**

- Data in transit: TLS/SSL encryption (HTTPS)  
- Data at rest: AES-256 encryption for sensitive data  
- Passwords: Bcrypt hashing (we never see your actual password)

**Access Controls:**

- Role-based access (employees see only what they need)  
- Two-factor authentication for administrative accounts  
- Regular access audits and revocations

**Infrastructure:**

- Secure cloud hosting with redundancy  
- Regular security updates and patches  
- Automated backups (encrypted)  
- Intrusion detection and monitoring

**Development:**

- Security code reviews  
- Penetration testing (at scale)  
- Vulnerability scanning  
- Secure development practices

### 5.2 Your Responsibilities

**Protect Your Account:**

- Use strong, unique password  
- Don't share your password  
- Log out on shared devices  
- Enable two-factor authentication (when available)  
- Report suspicious activity immediately

### 5.3 Data Breach Notification

If data breach occurs:

- We'll notify affected users within 72 hours  
- Notification includes: what data affected, when it occurred, what we're doing  
- Posted on Platform and sent via email  
- Reported to authorities as required by law

**No security is perfect.** We cannot guarantee absolute security but commit to industry-standard practices and transparent breach response.
