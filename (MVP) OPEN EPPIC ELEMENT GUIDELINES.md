# **OPEN EPPIC ELEMENT GUIDELINES**

**Version:** 2.0  
 **Last Updated:** December 2025

---

## **WHAT IS AN ELEMENT?**

An Element is an **original, unique, detailed story component** that other creators can use in their Works.

**Element Types:**

* **Characters** \- Entities with agency (humans, aliens, gods, AI, personified animals)  
* **Settings** \- Locations at any scale (planets, cities, buildings, rooms, dimensions)  
* **Systems** \- Collections of rules (magic systems, governance, physics, technology)  
* **Items** \- Objects directed by characters (weapons, artifacts, tools, documents)  
* **Plots** \- Highly specific story structures (not generic archetypes)

---

## **ELEMENT REQUIREMENTS**

To register an Element, you must meet ALL four requirements:

### **1\. ORIGINAL**

Created by you, not copied from copyrighted works.

**Allowed:** Your original creations, transformative public domain versions (200+ unique words)  
 **Not Allowed:** Direct copies of copyrighted characters/settings, minor modifications of others' IP

### **2\. UNIQUE**

Distinctive enough to be recognizable.

**Allowed:** "Detective Sarah Chen," "The Crimson Archive," "The Threefold Path"  
 **Not Allowed:** "A Detective," "A Magical Library," "Magic"

**Test:** Can someone describe your Element specifically, or just say "it's like every other \[type\]"?

### **3\. DETAILED**

**Minimum:** 200 words of unique description

**Include:** Physical traits, personality/function, history, abilities/limitations, relationships/interactions

### **4\. DEFINED**

Clear boundaries so derivative creators understand guidelines of use if they desire to align with what is considered to be canon.

**Clarify:** What it can/cannot do, where/when it exists, restrictions, interactions, whether it can be destroyed/modified

---

## **MAJOR VS. MINOR ELEMENTS**

### **Major Elements (one or more applies):**

* Named consistently in original work  
* Multiple appearances across work  
* Significant plot impact or character arc  
* Featured in marketing/merchandise

### **Minor Elements:**

* Background or incidental mentions  
* Unnamed or generic descriptions  
* Limited plot function  
* Not merchandised

**Special Interest Tag:** Minor Elements can be tagged for filtering. Keeps libraries searchable while allowing interesting background Elements.

---

## **PUBLIC DOMAIN ELEMENTS**

**Platform maintains curated library** of verified public domain content (Shakespeare, Greek mythology, fairy tales, historical figures).

**You CAN register:** Detailed transformative versions with 200+ unique words  
 **You CANNOT register:** Generic public domain Elements as-is

---

## **ELEMENT COMMITMENT PERIODS**

### **Minimum Commitment: 1 Year**

Elements commit for 1 year minimum. Protects derivative creators who invest time/creativity.

All derivative works created with original elements gain a perpetual license and will remain on the platform even if original elements are removed. Derivative elements will also remain, but they cannot be used in any new works or elements.

---

## **ELEMENT USAGE & CANON STATUS**

### **Critical Policy Stance**

**CANON STATUS IS NOT A MODERATION CONCERN**

Derivative creators have creative freedom within Content Guidelines. Element Owners do NOT have:

* Veto power over derivative story choices  
* Ability to enforce "canonical accuracy"  
* Special protections beyond platform-wide policy  
* Right to remove content for being "wrong interpretation"

**If you're uncomfortable with derivatives having creative freedom within Content Guidelines, do not register Elements on Open Eppic.**

### **What You DO Control**

* Content Guidelines (platform enforces for everyone)  
* Owner-provided description (usage guidance)

### **What You DON'T Control**

* Story choices derivatives make  
* Character relationships or development  
* Violence/harm to Elements (within Content Guidelines)  
* Creative interpretations you dislike

## **DERIVATIVE ELEMENTS**

**Derivative Elements** build upon existing original Elements. They exist for community formation and discovery.

**Examples:**

* "Vampire Harry Potter" (based on Harry Potter)  
* "Detective in Middle-earth" (based on LOTR setting)  
* "Cyberpunk Magic System" (based on existing magic system)

### **Why Create Derivative Elements?**

* Works using your derivative cluster together  
* Creators discover your unique interpretation  
* Build reputation as creative transformer  
* Create Works using your derivative interpretation

### **Requirements**

1. Identify original sources (no nesting derivatives)  
2. Write 200+ unique words describing transformation  
3. Acknowledge connection to original sources

## **ELEMENT DESCRIPTIONS**

**Owner-Provided (editable anytime):**

* Your usage guidelines and notes  
* Helps derivative creators understand vision  
* Can be freely updated

## **REMOVAL PROCESS**

### **Standard Removal (at commitment expiration)**

**Soft Removal:**

* Element removed from library (new uses blocked)  
* Existing Works keep perpetual license

### **Safety Emergency Removal (RARE)**

**Requirements:**

* Genuine safety threat (harassment, stalking, doxxing, credible danger)  
* Evidence submitted to guardians@openeppic.com  
* Constitutional Guardian approval within 72 hours

**This exception requires genuine safety threat, not creative disagreement.**

## **BEST PRACTICES**

### **Before Registering**

**Ask yourself:**

* Am I comfortable with others interpreting my Element?  
* Am I okay with derivative creative freedom (within Content Guidelines)?  
* Will I keep this available for at least 1 year?  
* Is my Element sufficiently developed (200+ words)?

**If uncertain, wait.**

### **Writing Descriptions**

**Do:** Focus on core identity, provide concrete examples, include limitations, write clearly, leave room for interpretation

**Don't:** Try to control every use case, write overly restrictive guidelines, assume unstated knowledge

### **Managing Derivatives**

**Do:** Trust Content Guidelines, report genuine policy violations, engage positively if you choose

**Don't:** Enforce "canonical accuracy," report derivatives for creative choices you dislike, expect veto power, remove rashly

## **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

**Can I change my Element description after registration?**  
 Owner-provided description: yes, anytime.

**What if someone misuses my Element?**  
 Report Content Guideline violations to guardians@openeppic.com.

**Can I require derivatives to be "canonical"?**  
 No. Canon status is not a moderation concern.

**Can I remove my Element?**  
 Yes, after 1 year.

**Do existing Works using my Element remain published forever?**  
 Yes. Published Works have perpetual license even if you remove your Element.

**What if someone registers my Element before me?**  
 File DMCA notice if you own copyright.

**Can I use my Element outside Open Eppic?**  
 Yes. Open Eppic license is non-exclusive; you retain all rights.

**Can I register real people as Elements?**  
 Public domain historical figures with transformative description: yes. Living people: no (privacy/publicity rights).
