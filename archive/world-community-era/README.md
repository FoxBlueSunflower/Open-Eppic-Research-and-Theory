# Archive: World-Community Era

This directory preserves the earlier body of work on Open Eppic, kept for historical record rather than as current policy. It predates the project's shift to a Deming-informed, systems-thinking approach, and should be read as a snapshot of early thinking — not as an active spec, contract, or roadmap.

See the [root README](../../README.md) for the current mission and governance framing.

## Contents

### `MVP/`
Early MVP-scoped legal and product documents:
- `OPEN_EPPIC_CONSTITUTIONAL_CHARTER.md` — draft of the nine immutable principles
- `OPERATING_AGREEMENT.docx.md` — draft company structure and governance
- `TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md`, `OPEN_EPPIC_PRIVACY_POLICY.md`, `OPEN_EPPIC_CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md`, `OPEN_EPPIC_ELEMENT_GUIDELINES.md` — draft legal framework
- `CREATOR_GUIDE.md` — draft creator-facing guide
- `xano_schema_prompt.txt` — draft database schema (Xano-based backend)

### `Strategic/`
Later, more detailed drafts and strategic planning:
- Expanded versions of the constitutional charter, terms of service, privacy policy, content guidelines, and element guidelines
- `OPERATING_AGREEMENT.md` — expanded operating agreement draft
- `OPEN_EPPIC_LEGAL_RELATIONSHIP_BETWEEN_ORIGINAL_AND_DERIVATIVE_CREATORS.md`
- `Open_Eppic_Creator_Guide.md`, `Open_Eppic_Monetization_Guide.md`
- `OPEN_EPPIC_DEFERRED_FEATURES_&_DECISIONS.md` — decision log of deferred features and tradeoffs

### `Why_it_Matters`
The original case for why Open Eppic should exist.
