# OPEN EPPIC CONTENT GUIDELINES

**Version:** 2.0  
**Last Updated:** December 2025

---

## CORE PRINCIPLE

**Open Eppic allows wide creative freedom within moral and legal boundaries.**

We welcome:

- Diverse viewpoints and controversial ideas  
- Mature storytelling and dark themes  
- Political, religious, and philosophical content  
- Violence within story context  
- Complex moral questions

We prohibit:

- Content that dwells on graphic details  
- Sexual assault (all depictions)  
- Sexualizing minors  
- Hate speech and dehumanization  
- Harassment and illegal content

**Guiding Rule**: Don't dwell. Don't relish. Don't get creative with graphic details.

---

## MODERATION PHILOSOPHY

### We Curate, We Don't Create

Open Eppic curates a community of creators and moderates content to enforce Constitutional Commitments. However:

**We do NOT:**

- Author, create, or materially contribute to user content  
- Judge creative quality or canonical accuracy  
- Enforce "correct" interpretations of Elements  
- Remove content based on taste or ideology

**We DO:**

- Enforce safety and appropriateness standards  
- Remove content violating Content Guidelines  
- Exercise contextual judgment (not just keyword matching)  
- Protect constitutional principles (human dignity, protection of vulnerable)

### Hybrid Human \+ AI Moderation

**Automated Flagging:**

- Hive.ai (or equivalent) flags prohibited keywords  
- AI cannot judge context or nuance alone

**Constitutional Guardian Review:**

- Human review within 24 hours  
- Contextual evaluation considering story purpose  
- Distinction between depicting evil vs. glorifying evil  
- Documented precedents (case law approach)

### Transparency and Appeals

**All moderation decisions:**

- Explained on Constitutional Transparency Blog (anonymized)  
- Open to appeal with additional context  
- Precedents documented for future guidance

**We acknowledge imperfection:**

- Moderation involves judgment calls  
- Good faith efforts, publicly acknowledge shortcomings  
- Accept appeals, reconsider decisions

## STRICTLY PROHIBITED CONTENT

The following content will be removed immediately and may result in account termination:

### 1\. SEXUALIZING MINORS

**Zero tolerance.** Any sexual content involving characters under 18:

**Prohibited:**

- Sexual or romantic relationships between minors and adults  
- Descriptions of minors' bodies in sexual context  
- Sexual situations involving minors (even implied/off-screen)  
- "Aging up" canonically minor characters for sexual content

**This includes:**

- Characters who are minors in original work (even if "aged up" in your story)  
- Characters presented as minors (regardless of stated age)  
- Any sexual content with characters appearing under 18

**No exceptions.** This is constitutionally protected and non-negotiable.

### 2\. EXPLICIT SEXUAL CONTENT

**Prohibited:**

- Explicit descriptions of sexual acts  
- Descriptions of genitalia or sexual arousal  
- Creative descriptions of nakedness in sexual contexts  
- Dwelling on sexual details or mechanics  
- Sexual language (fuck, cunt, and similar terms used sexually)

**Allowed:**

- Romance and attraction between adults  
- Kissing, embracing, emotional intimacy  
- Physical excitement (racing hearts, breathlessness) without explicit detail  
- "Fade to black" approach (scene ends before explicit acts begin)  
- Brief, non-sexual mentions of nakedness (e.g., "a naked person streaked across the neighborhood")  
- Married/committed couples implied to have intimate relationships

**The Line:** Once you start describing bodies in arousal or mechanics of sexual acts, you've crossed into forbidden territory. Keep it above PG-13/R-rated mainstream fiction level.

### 3\. SEXUAL ASSAULT / RAPE

**ALL DEPICTIONS PROHIBITED** \- including off-screen, implied, and "fade-to-black."

**Prohibited:**

- On-screen assault (any description)  
- Off-screen assault (door closes, implied)  
- "Fade-to-black" rape scenarios  
- Detailed aftermath discussions that describe the assault  
- Threats of sexual assault as plot device

**Allowed:**

- Acknowledgment that character survived sexual assault (in their past)  
- Stories about trauma recovery and healing (without depicting assault)  
- Justice and accountability for perpetrators (without depicting assault)  
- Discussions of consent and healthy relationships

**Why This Absolute Ban:**

While sexual assault is a serious literary topic, the maturity level and deep discomfort this subject creates makes it unsuitable for Open Eppic's collaborative environment.

**Collaborative storytelling requires baseline comfort.** When Element Owners share their characters, they trust the community to treat them respectfully. Sexual assault crosses a line that makes collaboration untenable.

**Good stories can address trauma, recovery, and justice without depicting the assault itself.**

### 4\. GRAPHIC VIOLENCE

**Prohibited:**

- Detailed descriptions of torture, mutilation, or suffering  
- Dwelling on gore, blood, or injury mechanics  
- Relishing creative descriptions of how violence unfolds  
- "Torture porn" \- violence for shock value or gratification  
- Extensive descriptions of pain and suffering

**Allowed:**

- Combat and action scenes with realistic consequences  
- Brief, matter-of-fact descriptions of violence  
- Character death (permanent or temporary)  
- Violence that serves story without dwelling on graphic details  
- Injury, bleeding, broken bones (brief descriptions)  
- Characters dealing with trauma, PTSD, recovery

**The Line:**

The difference is in **dwelling**.

**Example \- ALLOWED:** "The blade struck true. He gasped, blood spreading across his shirt, and fell. She stood over him, breathing hard, knowing she had no choice."

**Example \- PROHIBITED:** "The blade entered just below the ribcage, twisting as it pierced the lung, warm blood bubbling from the wound as he struggled to breathe, each gasp more desperate, his eyes wide with terror as he felt his life draining..."

**Test**: Does the description serve the story, or are you getting creative with graphic details? If the latter, cut it.

### 5\. HATE SPEECH & DEHUMANIZATION

**Core Principle**: ALL humans are made in God's image with immense worth. Hate, dehumanization, or calls for violence against ANY person violates Open Eppic's foundational commitment to Human Dignity.

**Prohibited:**

- Dehumanizing language toward ANY person or group (comparing humans to animals, vermin, objects)  
- Slurs or epithets targeting ANY characteristic (race, religion, gender, nationality, disability, sexual orientation, etc.)  
- Calls for violence, harm, or discrimination against ANY person or group  
- Endorsing hate ideologies that deny inherent dignity of any human being

**Critical Distinction:**

- We can hate what people DO (actions, choices, evil behavior)  
- We cannot hate what people ARE (their humanity, worth, potential for redemption)  
- Even history's worst people are created in God's image

**In Stories:**

**Allowed:**

- Villains being hateful, bigoted, cruel (depicting ≠ endorsing)  
- Characters using slurs or expressing prejudice (showing character flaws)  
- Historical contexts with period-accurate bigotry  
- Exploring consequences of hate and prejudice

**Prohibited:**

- Story endorsing or celebrating dehumanization  
- Glorifying hate ideologies  
- Narrative voice using dehumanizing language uncritically

**The Line:** Show that it's wrong, show consequences, or remain neutral—but don't glorify hate.

### 6\. HARASSMENT & TARGETED ATTACKS

**Prohibited:**

- Using platform to harass, stalk, or threaten specific individuals  
- Creating content designed to mock, shame, or humiliate real people  
- Doxxing (sharing private information without consent)  
- Coordinated attacks or brigading  
- Targeted harassment campaigns

**Note**: This applies to real people, not fictional characters. Villains can be as villainous as the story requires.

### 7\. ILLEGAL CONTENT

**Prohibited:**

- Instructions for illegal activities (bomb-making, drug manufacturing, hacking, etc.)  
- Copyright infringement (using others' copyrighted characters without permission)  
- Content violating US federal or Kansas state law  
- Fraudulent or deceptive content  
- Content facilitating illegal transactions

**Note**: Depicting illegal acts in fiction (theft, murder, drug use) is allowed. Instructions for committing illegal acts in real life are prohibited.

## EXPLICITLY ALLOWED CONTENT

Open Eppic welcomes diverse perspectives and mature storytelling within the bounds above.

### Political & Ideological Content

**Allowed:**

- All political viewpoints (left, right, libertarian, anarchist, etc.)  
- Satire and political commentary  
- Stories exploring political systems, revolutions, governance  
- Controversial ideas and thought experiments  
- Critique of governments, institutions, ideologies

### Religious & Philosophical Content

**Allowed:**

- Stories featuring religious themes, debates, characters  
- Exploration of faith, doubt, conversion, deconversion  
- Philosophical questions about morality, existence, purpose  
- Critique or defense of religious beliefs  
- Atheist, agnostic, or skeptical perspectives  
- Exploration of different religions and spiritual traditions

### Dark & Mature Themes

**Allowed:**

- Addiction and substance abuse (handled respectfully)  
- Physical/emotional abuse and trauma (not glorified)  
- Mental illness, depression, anxiety, PTSD  
- Suicide ideation and self-harm (not glorified or instructional)  
- War, genocide, historical atrocities (contextual, not gratuitous)  
- Moral ambiguity and ethical dilemmas  
- Unhappy endings, tragic outcomes  
- Evil characters doing evil things (depicting ≠ endorsing)

### Violence in Context

**Allowed:**

- Combat, warfare, battles, duels  
- Crime stories (murder, theft, assault as plot elements)  
- Horror elements (suspense, fear, supernatural threats)  
- Physical harm (injury, death, dismemberment) with brief descriptions  
- Consequences of violence (grief, guilt, trauma, recovery)  
- Revenge, justice, moral complexity

### Controversial & Uncomfortable Ideas

**Allowed:**

- Challenging social norms or taboos  
- Exploring unpopular or minority viewpoints  
- Dystopian or authoritarian systems (as storytelling)  
- Content some find offensive but doesn't violate policies  
- Difficult questions without easy answers

### Language

**Allowed:**

- Non-sexual profanity: shit, damn, hell, bastard, ass, bitch

**Prohibited:**

- Sexual profanity used sexually: fuck, cunt  
- Slurs targeting humans based on identity (race, religion, etc.)

**Context Matters:**

- "Fuck" as general expletive (not sexual) \= borderline, use sparingly  
- "Fuck" in sexual context \= prohibited

## Critical Distinction:

Open Eppic allows depicting difficult realities within story context. Open Eppic prohibits encouraging harmful behaviors.

#### **Framework:**

| Content Type | DEPICTING (Allowed) | ENCOURAGING (Prohibited) |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Suicide** | Character struggles with suicidal thoughts; story explores consequences, grief, impact on others | Content romanticizing suicide, portraying as solution or escape, providing specific methods |
| **Self-Harm** | Character's self-harm shown as symptom of deeper pain, mental illness, trauma | Content glorifying self-harm, presenting as healthy coping, instructional details |
| **Eating Disorders** | Character battles ED; realistic recovery journey with setbacks | Pro-ana/pro-mia content; "thinspiration"; tips/tricks for restriction; competitive weight loss |
| **Body Dysmorphia** | Character struggles with body image; explores societal pressure | Content reinforcing dysmorphia as valid perception; encouraging unhealthy body goals |
| **Addiction** | Character's addiction shown realistically with consequences | Content glorifying substance abuse, minimizing risks, instructional drug use |

#### **Enforcement Guidance:**

**Test for "Encouraging":**

1. Does the narrative voice or story structure present harmful behavior as desirable?  
2. Does the content provide specific instructions for harmful acts?  
3. Are harmful behaviors portrayed without consequence or as solutions?  
4. Would vulnerable readers interpret this as advocacy rather than depiction?

## CANON STATUS IS NOT A MODERATION CONCERN

**CRITICAL PRINCIPLE:**

Platform moderates for safety and appropriateness, NOT for creative quality or canonical consistency.

### What This Means

**Derivative creators may:**

- Write non-canonical stories, alternate universes, "what-if" scenarios  
- Depict violence, harm, or death to Elements (within Content Guidelines)  
- Explore Element relationships, personality, development  
- Make creative choices Element Owners might not make  
- Create content Element Owners might dislike (as long as policy-compliant)

**Element Owners do NOT have:**

- Veto power over derivative story choices  
- Ability to enforce "canonical accuracy"  
- Special protections beyond platform-wide policy  
- Right to remove content just because it's "wrong" interpretation

**Element Owners DO have:**

- Right to report Content Guideline violations  
- Right to remove Elements at commitment expiration (new uses blocked, existing stay)  
- Right to keep or forfeit earnings from existing Works

### One Policy for All Creators

All creators—Element Owners and derivative creators alike—follow the same Content Guidelines.

**There is no "Element Owner tier" with special content protections.**

**If Element Owner uncomfortable with derivatives having creative freedom (within Content Guidelines), they should not register Elements on Open Eppic.**

## CONTENT WARNINGS & LABELS

**Recommended but not required:**

Creators should use content warnings for:

- Violence (even non-graphic)  
- Sexual content (even "fade to black")  
- Dark themes (abuse, addiction, suicide, trauma)  
- Strong language  
- Potentially triggering content

**Content warnings do NOT excuse policy violations.**

Even with warnings, content must comply with Content Guidelines.

## ENFORCEMENT & APPEALS

### How We Moderate

**User Reports:**

- Community members can report content they believe violates policies  
- Report reasons limited to specific policy violations  
- "I disagree" or "I dislike this" is NOT a valid report reason

**AI Flagging:**

- Automated keyword detection via Hive.ai  
- Flags content for human review (does not auto-remove)

**Constitutional Guardian Review:**

- Manual review within 24 hours  
- Contextual evaluation considering story purpose  
- Good faith standard (we enforce in good faith, not perfection)

### Consequences for Violations

**First Offense (Minor Violation):**

- Warning \+ request to edit content  
- Opportunity to fix before removal  
- No earnings forfeited if fixed promptly

**Serious Violation:**

- Content removed within 24 hours  
- Warning issued  
- Creator may appeal

**Repeat Violations:**

- Second violation within 12 months: Account suspended, all earnings from violating content forfeited  
- Third violation: Permanent account termination

**Severe Violations (Immediate Ban):**

- Sexual content involving minors (zero tolerance)  
- Repeated copyright infringement (3+ DMCA violations)  
- Harassment or doxxing  
- No warnings, immediate termination

### Impact on Derivatives

**If Element violates Content Guidelines and is removed:**

**Non-Violating Derivative Works:**

- Remain public  
- Creators get 90 days notice to remove dependency on violating Element  
- After 90 days, Works using violating Element archived (not deleted)  
- Creators can restore by reworking to remove Element

**Violating Derivative Works:**

- Reviewed individually  
- If derivative violates through endorsement/context: also removed  
- If derivative only violates by association: warning \+ 30 days to edit

**Monetization:**

- Creator of violating content: forfeits ALL earnings (past and future)  
- Non-violating derivative creators: keep past earnings, can continue if reworked in time

### Appeals Process

**If your content is removed or you receive warning:**

1. You may appeal within 30 days  
2. Email [appeals@openeppic.com](mailto:appeals@openeppic.com) with:  
   - Username and Work title  
   - Specific policy you believe was misapplied  
   - Context or evidence supporting your appeal  
3. Different moderator reviews your appeal  
4. Response within 7 business days  
5. If granted: content restored, warning removed

**Appeal Tips:**

- Provide context (full scene, not just flagged passage)  
- Explain story purpose and narrative intent  
- Distinguish depicting evil vs. glorifying evil  
- Show compliance with "don't dwell" standard  
- Be respectful (hostile appeals less likely to succeed)

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Can I write stories where bad things happen to characters?** A: Yes. Villains can be villainous. Bad things happen in stories. Depicting ≠ endorsing.

**Q: Can I write about controversial topics?** A: Yes. We protect diverse viewpoints. Content isn't removed because it's controversial or offensive.

**Q: What if Element Owner doesn't like how I depicted their Element?** A: That's not a moderation concern. Canon status is creative choice, not policy violation.

**Q: Can I write about trauma survivors?** A: Yes. You can write about recovery, healing, justice. You cannot depict the assault itself.

**Q: Is all violence prohibited?** A: No. Combat, action, injury, death are allowed. Torture porn and dwelling on graphic details are prohibited.

**Q: Can characters curse?** A: Yes (non-sexual profanity). Avoid sexual cursing (fuck, cunt).

**Q: What if I'm unsure if my content crosses the line?** A: Ask yourself: Am I dwelling? Am I relishing? Am I getting creative with graphic details? If yes, revise.

**Q: Can I appeal if my content is removed?** A: Yes. Email [appeals@openeppic.com](mailto:appeals@openeppic.com) within 30 days with context and reasoning.

**Q: What if Element Owner reports my content as "non-canonical"?** A: We'll review for Content Guideline violations only. Canon disputes are not moderation issues.

**Q: Can I write unhappy endings?** A: Yes. Tragic outcomes, "bad guy wins," moral ambiguity—all allowed.

**Q: Are content warnings required?** A: Recommended but not required. They don't excuse policy violations.

## FINAL NOTE

These guidelines exist to protect the community while preserving creative freedom.

**When in doubt, ask yourself:**

- Am I dwelling on graphic details?  
- Am I relishing creative descriptions of violence or sexuality?  
- Does this serve the story, or is it gratuitous?  
- Would this make collaborative storytelling uncomfortable?

If the answer is yes to any, reconsider your approach.

**Open Eppic's mission**: Enable creators to collaborate and monetize while maintaining human dignity and creative freedom within moral boundaries.
