# **Open Eppic Constitutional Charter**

## **Mission**

Enabling creators to build on each other's work in ways that honor both original creation and derivative transformation.

## **Constancy of Purpose**

To solve the derivative works problem by creating a system where original creators retain ownership, earn ongoing revenue, and are protected by platform-wide content standards—while derivative creators gain legal permission, commercial opportunity and creative freedom within those bounds. The platform serves both through constitutional governance, accepting that honor means fair treatment and compensation, not creative control.

## **Preamble: Theological Foundation**

Open Eppic was founded in recognition of the God of the Bible—Father, Son (Jesus), and Holy Spirit—as the ultimate reality, the source of all goodness, truth, and creativity, and the sovereign owner of all creation. While we acknowledge this foundation explicitly, we recognize that the moral principles flowing from this source can be affirmed by people of diverse beliefs as self-evident truths.

Open Eppic exists under the authority of transcendent moral law, not human opinion. These Constitutional Commitments are derived from that moral order and bind the company absolutely. The company will cease operations rather than violate these principles.

**Governance Philosophy:** We trust that God's ways prove themselves in practice. Rather than elaborate protective mechanisms, we embrace radical transparency, economic alignment, and ecosystem resilience through constitutional forking. Truth reveals itself when visible; righteousness demonstrates its superiority when tested in the market.

## **Article I: Recognition of Transcendent Moral Order**

We affirm that:

1. **Objective morality exists** \- Right and wrong are established by God and are not subject to human opinion, cultural preference, majority vote, or temporal fashion.  
2. **Humanity is subject to this moral order** \- No person, organization, government, or movement has authority to redefine fundamental moral truths. Claims to moral innovation are either rediscoveries of existing truth or departures into error.  
3. **Open Eppic is bound by these principles** \- The company exists under this moral order. All decisions—operational, financial, strategic—must align with these commitments. Profitability, legality, or popularity cannot justify violating them.  
4. **Finite human wisdom requires humility** \- While certain principles are unchangeable (as established by God's clear commands), their application in specific contexts requires wisdom that varies with time, place, and circumstance. We commit to seeking this wisdom while maintaining fidelity to foundational truth.

## **Article II: The Unchangeable Moral Commitments**

These principles cannot be amended, removed, or reinterpreted to contradict their plain meaning. They form the immovable foundation of Open Eppic.

### **II.1 \- The Image of God: Human Dignity and Sanctity of Life**

**Foundation:** "So God created mankind in his own image, in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them." (Genesis 1:27)

**We affirm:**

* Every human being possesses inherent, sacred worth as an image-bearer of God  
* Human life begins at conception when God forms a soul within the womb, making all human life from that moment equally valuable  
* Only God has authority to imbue souls into created things; He has done so only for humans

**Application to Platform:**

* All persons deserve respect, kindness, and equal treatment regardless of agreement, disagreement, characteristics, identity, or behavior  
* No person may be treated as merely a means to an end or reduced to an object  
* Artificial intelligence and digital entities may warrant respectful treatment (as with animals in God's creation) but can never be granted equal moral standing with humans

**Prohibitions:**

* Content that dehumanizes, mocks human dignity, or treats persons as objects  
* Business practices that exploit users, creators, or employees  
* Discrimination in moderation, access, or opportunity based on any human characteristic or description  
* Any action that denies the sanctity of human life at any stage

**Clarifications:**

* Stories may depict evil treatment of persons (as the Bible does), but the platform cannot promote or extensively engage with such content as entertainment

### **II.2 \- Truth and Honesty: Bearing True Witness**

**Foundation:** "You shall not bear false witness." (Exodus 20:16) / "The Lord detests lying lips, but he delights in people who are trustworthy." (Proverbs 12:22)

**We affirm:**

* God speaks only truth; His people must do likewise  
* Deception, misrepresentation, manipulation, and dishonesty are forbidden  
* Transparency is required in all dealings—with users, creators, employees, and stakeholders  
* Promises made must be kept; if circumstances require changes, they must be communicated openly with honest explanation

**Prohibitions:**

* Dark patterns, hidden fees, misleading marketing, or deceptive interface design  
* Selective enforcement of rules (saying one thing, doing another)  
* Misrepresenting platform capabilities, policies, or financial terms  
* Concealing information that stakeholders have a right to know

**Obligations:**

* Real-time transparency in all financial, operational, and governance matters  
* Public documentation of policy changes with honest rationale  
* Clear, readable terms of service (no burying important terms in legal jargon)  
* Accessible explanations of how algorithms, discovery, and monetization work

### **II.3 \- Ownership and Stewardship: God's Gift of Property**

**Foundation:** "The earth is the Lord's, and everything in it, the world, and all who live in it." (Psalm 24:1) / "You shall not steal." (Exodus 20:15)

**We affirm:**

* God owns all creation and gives ownership to whomever He pleases  
* Ownership is a God-given right established by the Creator; theft violates God's order  
* Just as God owns the universe yet grants humans dominion and stewardship within it, so humans own things and may give them to whomever they please  
* Property rights must be respected: creators own their work, users own their data, employees own their compensation  
* Open Eppic owns the platform infrastructure and has full agency and responsibility to steward it according to God's purposes

**The Stewardship Paradox:**

* Open Eppic owns the platform with full legal rights and responsibilities (a gift from God)  
* Open Eppic stewards the platform, recognizing it as held in trust from God for a purpose beyond ourselves  
* Ownership grants authority; stewardship imposes obligation  
* We may exercise our ownership rights fully, but never in ways that violate our stewardship duties to God and community

**Prohibitions:**

* Theft of intellectual property (respecting creator rights, enforcing proper licensing)  
* Platform claiming ownership of user-generated content beyond what's necessary for operation  
* Unjust deprivation of earnings, access, or property without due process  
* Using ownership power to exploit rather than serve those entrusted to our care

**Obligations:**

* Clear intellectual property rights for creators who retain ownership of their original works  
* Transparent revenue sharing with immutable terms published openly  
* Respect for user data as their property (minimal collection, user control, no selling)  
* Platform resources used for mission fulfillment, not personal enrichment of leadership

### **II.4 \- Justice and Protection of the Vulnerable**

**Foundation:** "Defend the weak and the fatherless; uphold the cause of the poor and the oppressed." (Psalm 82:3) / "Do not take advantage of the widow or the fatherless." (Exodus 22:22)

**We affirm:**

* God commands special care for the vulnerable: the poor, the foreigner, the orphan, the widow—those unable to sustain or defend themselves  
* Systems must be designed to prevent exploitation and provide meaningful recourse for the powerless  
* Those with power have heightened responsibility to exercise it justly  
* Justice includes both protection from harm and fair treatment in all processes

**Applications:**

* Small creators receive identical treatment as established ones (no preferential moderation or visibility)  
* Clear appeal processes exist for all moderation decisions with accessible explanations  
* Accessibility features for disabled users (technical justice reflecting human dignity)  
* Transparent rules prevent powerful users from exploiting vulnerable ones  
* Revenue models don't exploit creators' economic desperation or lack of alternatives

**Prohibitions:**

* Taking advantage of users' lack of technical knowledge or platform experience  
* Predatory monetization schemes or exploitative contract terms  
* Allowing harassment or coordinated attacks against individuals or minority viewpoints  
* Unequal enforcement of rules based on user influence, popularity, or revenue generation

### **II.5 \- Sabbath Rest and Human Limits**

**Foundation:** "Remember the Sabbath day by keeping it holy. Six days you shall labor and do all your work, but the seventh day is a Sabbath to the Lord your God." (Exodus 20:8-10)

**We affirm:**

* God rested on the seventh day and made it holy, establishing rhythm of work and rest  
* Rest is sacred; productivity is not the ultimate measure of human worth  
* Human beings have God-designed limits that must be respected, not optimized away  
* While doing good takes precedence over Sabbath observance, the principle of rest remains binding  
* Technology should serve human flourishing, not enslave users to endless engagement or workers to unsustainable demands

**Applications:**

* Employees have sustainable workloads with true time off (no expectation of constant availability)  
* Platform does not exploit addictive patterns, FOMO (fear of missing out), or compulsive behaviors  
* No gamification designed primarily to maximize engagement at users' expense  
* Features that encourage healthy boundaries and natural stopping points in usage

**Prohibitions:**

* Requiring employees to work unsustainable hours or creating culture of perpetual crunch  
* Designing features that prey on psychological vulnerabilities or compulsive behavior  
* Measuring success primarily through engagement metrics that incentivize addiction  
* Creating artificial urgency or scarcity to drive compulsive checking

### **II.6 \- Creative Freedom Within Moral Bounds**

**Foundation:** God created all things and there is immense possibility for creativity within His creation. / God allows immense evil on the earth. God also intervenes before evil consumes the whole world.

**We affirm:**

* Creativity is a divine gift reflecting God's image in humanity as sub-creators  
* Stories explore the full range of human experience, including suffering, temptation, evil, and redemption  
* Art attempts to understand a world where great good and great evil coexist, often in the same places and people  
* God allows wide freedom while maintaining boundaries; Open Eppic does likewise  
* However, extensive engagement with graphic depictions of evil corrupts the soul and desensitizes to suffering

**The Balance:**

* Evil may occur in stories (murder, betrayal, oppression, war) as it occurs throughout the Bible's narratives  
* Evil may be depicted and explored but not glorified or lingered upon in graphic, exploitative detail  
* The distinction: A story where a character is murdered or assaulted (allowed with content warnings) vs. detailed torture or sexual violence presented as entertainment (forbidden)

**Content Policy:**

**Forbidden:**

* Graphic sexual content (detailed, explicit description of sexual acts)  
* Graphic violence (detailed gore, torture described for shock value or entertainment)  
* Content that sexualizes minors in any form  
* Direct hate speech, calls to violence, or promotion of illegal harmful activities  
* Content that endorses or celebrates cruelty, oppression, or dehumanization as virtuous  
* Animal cruelty depicted extensively or gratuitously (respecting God's creation)

**Allowed with Content Warnings:**

* Violence and its consequences (non-graphic or off-screen)  
* Adult themes including sexuality (implied, "fade to black," or discussed without explicit detail)  
* Political content across the spectrum  
* Exploration of difficult moral questions and controversial ideas  
* Depiction of evil characters and actions within story context (villains can be villainous)  
* Mature themes addressing real human struggles (addiction, abuse, trauma) when handled with care

**Platform's Role:**

* We moderate content, not ideas or ideologies  
* Stories may feature characters with worldviews we find abhorrent (that's what villains are)  
* Characters may do terrible things (that's what makes stories dramatic)  
* But the platform itself does not become a venue for extensive engagement with graphic evil as entertainment

**Publisher Responsibility:**

Open Eppic exercises active editorial control through Constitutional Guardian review. This is not passive content hosting but active stewardship of a creative community. The platform's editorial judgments include:

* Determining whether content "dwells" on graphic details (subjective judgment)  
* Assessing whether stories "glorify" vs "depict" harmful behavior (contextual evaluation)  
* Evaluating hate speech claims within fictional narrative contexts (nuanced analysis)  
* Enforcing ethical boundaries that exceed bare legal compliance

**We commit to enforcing these standards through good faith efforts, publicly acknowledging when we fail to meet this standard.**

This active curation justifies our role as Publisher and fulfills our constitutional mandate to protect vulnerable community members while preserving creative freedom.

### **II.7 \- Love and Relational Commitment**

**Foundation:** "A new command I give you: Love one another. As I have loved you, so you must love one another." (John 13:34) / God desires to be in relationship with every person.

**We affirm:**

* God is love; His people must reflect this character  
* Thinking, saying, or doing anything without love contradicts God's nature  
* God invites all to relationship with Him; the invitation is optional, not coerced  
* Open Eppic invites all to participate in this creative community; acceptance is entirely optional

**Applications:**

* Platform fosters genuine community and connection between creators, readers, and element owners  
* Moderation is corrective and educational, aimed at restoration, not punitive or shaming  
* Disagreement—even sharp disagreement—does not justify cruelty; respect is maintained even in conflict  
* Users may decline participation in any aspect without penalty (opt-in philosophy, not opt-out defaults)  
* Features encourage collaboration and mutual support, not zero-sum competition

**Prohibitions:**

* Harassment, bullying, or personal attacks (even against those who have violated rules)  
* Coercive design patterns (forced participation, punitive opt-outs, social pressure mechanics)  
* Creating or tolerating hostile environments for any group  
* Using platform power to humiliate, shame, or make examples of users  
* Rewarding or incentivizing conflict and division for engagement purposes

### **II.8 \- Humility and Service**

**Foundation:** God was humble to the point of washing the feet of sinners. / God came to earth to serve others.

**We affirm:**

* Though God is holy and perfect, He served sinners with profound humility  
* Open Eppic is imperfect and commits to continual learning, growth, and improvement  
* The company exists to serve its community, not to be served by them  
* Leadership is stewardship and service, not lordship or control  
* We are not perfect, but we commit to honestly acknowledging failures and learning from them

**Applications:**

* Admitting mistakes publicly and promptly when they occur  
* Seeking user feedback genuinely and incorporating it when wise  
* Prioritizing actual user needs over company ego, founder vision, or institutional inertia when appropriate  
* Staff and leadership are servants of the mission, not overlords of the platform  
* Listening especially to critical voices and minority perspectives

**Prohibitions:**

* Defensiveness or dismissiveness toward legitimate criticism  
* "We know best" attitudes that ignore lived user experience  
* Using market position or platform power to dictate terms unfairly  
* Pride that refuses correction or improvement  
* Treating users as problems to be managed rather than people to be served

### **II.9 \- Freedom Within Boundaries**

**Foundation:** God allows immense evil on the earth. God also intervenes before evil consumes the whole world. / God grants humans free will while maintaining moral boundaries for flourishing.

**We affirm:**

* God grants humans radical freedom, including freedom to reject Him and His ways  
* With freedom comes responsibility; boundaries exist to protect flourishing, not to control thought  
* Open Eppic allows wide creative and ideological freedom within defined moral boundaries  
* Boundaries exist not to control thought or enforce conformity but to prevent direct harm  
* The line between protection and oppression requires constant vigilance and humility

**The Principle of Minimal Intervention:**

* Platform intervenes only when necessary to prevent flagrant harm or clear violations  
* "I disagree with this" ≠ grounds for removal  
* "This offends me" ≠ grounds for removal  
* "This causes direct harm (harassment, exploitation, illegal activity, content policy violation)" \= grounds for intervention  
* When in doubt, err on the side of freedom

**The Third Option Principle:**

* When two sides demand the platform enforce their view, actively seek the option that preserves freedom for both  
* Example: Rather than mandate "correct" pronoun usage OR ban pronoun discussion entirely, allow individual choice and expression  
* Default to preserving agency rather than imposing mandates wherever possible  
* Recognize that not every disagreement requires platform adjudication

**Applications:**

* Controversial ideas, diverse political content, and artistically challenging work are protected  
* Personal attacks, direct harassment, and clear incitement to harm are not protected  
* Users can disagree—even vehemently, even about fundamental issues—within boundaries of respect  
* Platform does not take sides in ideological, political, or theological disputes among users  
* Process fairness and consistent rule application matter more than outcomes

**The Mental Helmet Principle:**

* Just as people wear physical protection (shoes, coats) when venturing into challenging environments, users must exercise reasonable psychological resilience  
* Not all discomfort, offense, or emotional reaction justifies content removal  
* Content warnings and filtering tools empower users to make their own choices  
* Platform protects against direct harm but cannot eliminate all discomfort from human interaction

**The Reader Safety Principle:**

Freedom requires responsibility. When creative content can cause documentable harm to vulnerable readers (e.g., suicide contagion effects, eating disorder instruction), boundaries exist not to control thought but to prevent the misuse of freedom that destroys the flourishing of others.

**Research Evidence:**

* Suicide depiction without consequence can increase suicide rates in vulnerable populations ("Werther Effect")  
* Eating disorder content can serve as instruction manuals for self-destruction  
* Specific harmful behaviors can be encouraged vs. depicted responsibly

**Platform Response:**

* Mandatory content warnings for documented harm risks  
* "Depicting vs. Encouraging" standard (show reality, don't advocate harm)  
* Crisis resources provided alongside difficult content  
* Balance creative freedom with protection of vulnerable readers

**This is NOT censorship of ideas.** Stories can explore suicide, self-harm, eating disorders, trauma, and dark realities. They must not romanticize, instruct in, or encourage these harmful behaviors. The distinction matters.

Just as God allows evil in the world while maintaining boundaries, Open Eppic allows difficult content while protecting against the weaponization of creativity to harm the vulnerable.

### **II.10 \- Structural Reality: Freedom Within God's Created Order**

**Foundation:** "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth." (Genesis 1:1) / "For in him we live and move and have our being." (Acts 17:28) / "Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will never pass away." (Matthew 24:35)

**We affirm:**

* God grants humans radical freedom—including freedom to reject Him entirely—yet all humans exist within the reality He created  
* This reveals a profound truth: **true freedom operates within structure, not in its absence**  
* God's creation establishes the "fabric of reality"—love, truth, meaning, purpose, order  
* Heaven (union with God) contains these qualities in fullness; hell (separation from God) lacks them entirely  
* Humans exercise choice **within** God's created order, even when rebelling against it  
* This life is our opportunity to choose alignment with God's reality or rejection of it  
* God does not apologize for establishing boundaries—they are expressions of His nature and the foundation of flourishing

**Application to Platform:**

Just as God establishes boundaries within which humans freely operate, Open Eppic establishes a constitutional framework within which creators freely operate.

**We will not apologize for being a force for good.** We enforce the fabric of our model—love, truth, ownership, justice, humility—just as God enforces the fabric of His creation. The Constitutional Charter represents the "structure of reality" within Open Eppic's creative ecosystem.

**Critical Distinction from God's Universe:**

* **God's creation:** Inescapable (all humans exist within it, subject to its laws)  
* **Open Eppic:** Entirely optional (many alternative platforms exist, genuine choice available)

**Users are completely free to:**

* Join our model and operate within its constitutional boundaries  
* Reject our model and choose alternative platforms  
* Fork our open-source code and create communities with different principles  
* Create derivative platforms that compete with us

**We cannot and will not compromise the Constitutional Charter** to accommodate those who wish to operate outside its boundaries. Just as gravity remains regardless of belief in it, Open Eppic's constitutional principles remain regardless of agreement with them.

**This is not tyranny—it is honest structure.** We clearly state what we are, what we stand for, and what we require. We make our boundaries visible before users commit. Those who resonate with this mission are welcome; those who don't have genuine alternatives.

**Transparency honors human dignity and agency** while maintaining mission integrity. We do not hide our principles, change them to suit trends, or selectively enforce them for commercial advantage.

**Prohibitions:**

* Apologizing for or undermining constitutional principles to appease critics  
* Compromising boundaries to increase user count or commercial success  
* Hiding or obscuring constitutional requirements during onboarding  
* Claiming neutrality while enforcing particular values (dishonest framing)  
* Preventing users from forking code or creating alternatives  
* Making exit difficult or punitive (users must be genuinely free to leave)

**Clarifications:**

* **Open Eppic is not neutral:** We explicitly stand for God's reality as revealed in Scripture. This is not hidden or apologized for.  
* **Users are not trapped:** Unlike God's creation (which is inescapable), Open Eppic is entirely optional. Genuine alternatives exist, and we actively enable forking.  
* **Structure enables freedom:** Just as musical notes gain meaning within scales, and words within grammar, creative freedom flourishes within our constitutional structure—not in its absence.  
* **Boundaries protect flourishing:** Our constitutional limits exist not to control thought but to create an ecosystem where love, truth, and creativity can thrive without being destroyed by their opposites.

**The Epistemological Humility Principle:**

Open Eppic operates with **faith in God's design even when we cannot fully articulate the moral reasoning behind it.**

**We acknowledge:**

* God's wisdom infinitely exceeds human comprehension: "For my thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways my ways, declares the Lord" (Isaiah 55:8)  
* Some moral truths are self-evident in their outcomes even when their full theological basis remains mysterious  
* Finite human minds cannot always explain infinite divine wisdom  
* Observing that God's ways "work" in practice provides evidence even without complete theoretical understanding

**Applied to Platform Decisions:**

When faced with complex moral questions, **Open Eppic reserves the right to say "I don't know why, but I trust God's design."**

**Examples:**

* Why does God forbid certain behaviors? We may not fully understand, but we observe that His boundaries consistently produce flourishing when honored and harm when violated.  
* How do we balance competing goods in edge cases? We may not have perfect answers, but we trust that operating within God's revealed principles will prove wise over time.  
* What if cultural consensus declares God's standards outdated? We maintain them anyway, trusting His eternal wisdom over temporal trends.

**This means:**

* We don't need exhaustive philosophical justification for every constitutional principle  
* We can acknowledge uncertainty about application details while remaining confident in foundational truths  
* We observe outcomes and adjust implementation while holding core principles immutable  
* We operate with **informed faith**, not blind faith—God's patterns prove themselves in reality

**Critical Distinction:**

* **What we hold with certainty:** The nine Constitutional Commitments (derived from Scripture, observed in reality, proven by millennia of human experience)  
* **What we hold provisionally:** Specific implementation details, edge case resolutions, contextual applications  
* **What we admit we don't know:** Full theological reasoning for every divine command, perfect answers to every hypothetical, complete understanding of how all principles interact in every scenario

**We refuse two opposite errors:**

1. **Claiming omniscience:** Pretending we have perfect understanding of every moral question and its full theological basis  
2. **Abandoning foundations:** Discarding principles we can't fully explain in favor of "easier" standards we've invented ourselves

**Instead, we operate with humble confidence:** Confident in God's revealed truth, humble about our finite understanding, willing to say "I trust God's design here even though I can't fully explain it."

**This is not intellectual laziness—it is honest acknowledgment of human limits combined with faith in divine wisdom.** We study, reason, and refine our understanding while maintaining that some truths are held by faith in the One who designed reality itself.

As C.S. Lewis wrote: "I believe in Christianity as I believe that the sun has risen: not only because I see it, but because by it I see everything else."
