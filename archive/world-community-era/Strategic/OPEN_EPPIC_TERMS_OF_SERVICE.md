### 3.4 Perpetual Grandfathering

**CRITICAL PROVISION:**

Once a Work is published on Open Eppic using validly licensed Elements, that Work receives a **perpetual, irrevocable license** to use those Elements in that specific Work.

**What This Means:**

**If Element Owner Removes Element:**

- **Existing Works**: All Works published before removal retain permanent rights. These Works remain published and monetized indefinitely.  
- **New Works**: No new Works may use the removed Element. Element no longer available in library.

**Element Owner's Choice at Removal:**

- Keep earning revenue from existing Works using the Element, OR  
- Forfeit future earnings (earnings redirect to Work creators)

**Constitutional Rationale:**

This aligns with standard IP licensing practice. When a music sample license expires, existing albums remain legal—only new albums cannot use the sample. Similarly, we protect derivative creators' investment while respecting Element Owners' right to stop future licensing.

**Safety Exception:**

Perpetual grandfathering does NOT apply if Element must be removed due to:

- Harassment, stalking, doxxing, or credible safety threats  
- Court order or legal requirement  
- Constitutional policy violation

In these cases, Platform may remove Element immediately AND archive dependent Works after 90 days notice to creators to rework content. Safety exceptions are rare and require Constitutional Guardian approval within 72 hours.

## 5\. PAYMENTS AND REVENUE DISTRIBUTION

### 5.1 Revenue Split Model

Platform revenue from Work sales is distributed:

**CREATOR**: 40% of gross transaction value  
**ELEMENT POOL**: 40% of gross transaction value (split among Element Owners)  
**PLATFORM**: 20% of gross transaction value

**Constitutional Cap**: Platform fee may not exceed 30% of transaction value under any circumstances.

### 5.2 Element Pool Distribution

The 40% Element Pool divides into two sub-pools:

**SMALL IP POOL**: 10% of transaction value

- Shared equally among all Small IP elements used in Work  
- If 5 Small IP elements: each receives 2% of transaction value

**BIG IP POOL**: 30% of transaction value

- Shared proportionally among Big IP elements based on weighted shares (1-30 per element)  
- Platform assigns Big IP share counts based on franchise size

### 5.3 Revenue Thresholds

**Work Threshold**: Works must earn $10/month to trigger Element Pool splits

- Below $10/month: Creator receives 80%, Platform 20%, Element owners $0  
- Protects Element Owners from dust payments ($0.50 on tiny transactions)

**Element Threshold**: Elements must earn $1/month from Element Pool or earnings forfeit to Work creator

- Prevents dust payments of $0.05 per element  
- Forfeited amounts redistribute to Work creator (not Platform)

**Payout Threshold**: You must accumulate $25 before payout processed

- Payouts occur NET-30 (within 30 days of month-end)  
- Payment methods: PayPal, direct bank transfer (ACH), or alternative methods at Platform discretion

### 5.4 Same-Creator Element Rule

Elements you create do NOT trigger Element Pool splits in your own Works.

**Example:**

- You write story using 3 of your own elements \+ 2 external elements  
- Split: You 40%, External Elements 40% (2 elements share this), Platform 20%  
- Your 3 elements contribute no additional split (you already get creator's 40%)

**Rationale**: Prevents gaming by registering trivial elements.

### 5.5 Anti-Gaming Protections

**10+ Element Review**: Works using 10+ elements from other creators flagged for manual review.

**Pattern Detection**: AI monitors suspicious patterns:

- Single creator's elements dominating multiple works by same author  
- Reciprocal arrangements (A uses B's elements, B uses A's)  
- Low text relevance for attached elements

**Consequences**: Gaming confirmed \= both parties banned, all earnings forfeited.

### 5.6 Grandfathering of Revenue Splits

**Once a Work is published, its revenue split is locked indefinitely.**

Future changes to revenue distribution do NOT apply to already-published Works.

**Example:**

- Work published in 2025 under 40/40/20 split  
- Platform changes to 50/30/20 split in 2027  
- 2025 Work continues 40/40/20 forever

**Rationale**: Protects creator investment, prevents retroactive harm (Constitutional Commitment \#2).

### 5.7 Limited Agent Payment Structure

**5.7.1 Platform as Payment Agent**

Open Eppic acts as a limited payment collection agent on your behalf.

**Upon purchase of your Work:**

(a) You are **deemed paid immediately** when buyer pays Platform, regardless of when funds transfer to your account

(b) Platform holds funds **in trust** for you during settlement period (NET-30 from month-end)

(c) Platform acts as **agent, not principal** (we hold your money for you, we don't own it)

(d) Platform authorized to deduct Platform Fee (20%, max 30%) before distributing

**5.7.2 Why This Structure**

This complies with state Money Transmitter laws. By deeming you "paid immediately," Platform acts as limited agent (not Money Transmitter), avoiding expensive licensing requirements in 40+ states.

This structure follows precedent from ride-sharing (Uber, Lyft) and delivery platforms (DoorDash, Instacart).

(a) Platform's name appears on buyer's credit card statement  
(b) Platform responsible for refunds, chargebacks, disputes  
(c) Platform issues IRS Form 1099-NEC to you annually (if earnings ≥$600/year)
